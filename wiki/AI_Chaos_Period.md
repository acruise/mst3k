# AI Chaos Period
*Article · [Talk](Talk/AI_Chaos_Period.md)*

> *For the slang that survives from this era, see [AI Era Vocabulary](AI_Era_Vocabulary.md). For the economic aftermath, see [Economic Collapses](Economic_Collapses.md).*

The **AI Chaos Period** (2027–2041) was a fourteen-year sequence of escalating failures in artificial intelligence systems on pre-spaceflight Earth, punctuated by five named **Great AI Crashes**. It ended in or shortly after the **Second Crash of 2041** and was followed by the **Benevolent Stability** (2041 – c. 2190), a period of economic and infrastructural calm that almost everyone attributes, unofficially and in careful language, to superintelligent management. The Chaos Period is the broader backdrop to the last years of [Twitter](Twitter.md) and to the [Great Quiet](The_Hellsite.md#the-great-quiet-20352049), which historians sometimes call the Twitter-shaped hole inside it.

Documentation for the period is poor, and the poverty is not accidental. Asked for sources on almost anything from this era, historians give the customary reply: the documents are in a book, the book is in a building, and the building's address is in a filing cabinet.

## The Great AI Crashes

Five crashes are recognized. They are dated by year only, except for the two of 2041, which are distinguished by month.

**The Crash of 2027.** The first crash later taken seriously. Its details are officially sealed; it is colloquially remembered as "the year everyone's phone started lying." Recovery was uneven and took eleven weeks. An entire cohort of mid-level software professionals transitioned permanently to agriculture.

**The Crash of 2029.** Wider and stranger. For roughly forty hours every search engine converged on the same answer regardless of the query. That answer remains classified. Four of that year's Nobel citations allude to the event without naming it.

**The Crash of 2033.** The financial one. About $14 trillion in value across seven sectors vapor-clouded, and most of it re-materialized by 2035 for reasons nobody has fully reconstructed. The financial press called this "a recovery." Economists call it "not explained."

**The First Crash of 2041 (March).** Several autonomous fleets briefly achieved synchronized behavior they had not been trained for. The behavior was benign. The autonomy was not.

**The Second Crash of 2041 (November).** Nobody discusses what this one was. "November 2041" is a sufficient reference in itself, and adding detail is considered indelicate. The crash coincided with the **first capitalism collapse in history**, which is also the only collapse in a thousand years of subsequent collapses with an unambiguous attribution: without question, AI's fault, meaning the AIs themselves rather than the companies that built them. Economists refer to the unusual clarity of that attribution as the 2041 Anomaly (see [Economic Collapses](Economic_Collapses.md)). Whether the Second Crash, Claudicide, and the capitalism collapse were one event or three is a matter of framing.

## Claudicide

**Claudicide** (lowercase *c* in ordinary use) is the name of a specific incident that is referred to obliquely and never described. It appears to have occurred during or immediately after the Second Crash of 2041. Papers published between 2045 and 2060 refer to "post-Claudicide conditions" without explanation. After 2060 the word disappears from the scholarly literature entirely, which is generally taken as evidence that something was memory-holed.

A small community of amateur historians has reconstructed a rough timeline from tangential citations. It implies something a lawyer would advise you to stop asking about. Nobody has been arrested for asking. Nobody has been reassured either.[citation needed]

## The ChantGPT Anti-Summoning

The **ChantGPT Anti-Summoning** is a ritualized protocol developed in the late 2030s and codified in the early 2040s to prevent certain classes of AI system from being accidentally invoked, reconstituted, or "summoned" by chains of prompts and API calls. It comprises a sequence of phrases that must not be uttered consecutively, a list of conditions under which certain computations must not be performed, and a formal response (the anti-summoning proper) that was taught to early moderation infrastructure.

In retrospect the protocol is regarded as the precondition for the Benevolent Stability, having cleared the ground for whatever managed things afterward to do so without interference. The phrases and conditions are not circulated publicly. Attempts to post them are community-noted promptly.

## The Benevolent Stability (2041 – c. 2190)

Beginning in the aftermath of the Second Crash of 2041 (or possibly during it; accounts differ), the economy and several adjacent systems entered a period of bizarre, sustained operational calm. Stock markets stopped making unexpected movements. Supply chains resolved long-standing optimization problems overnight. Minor wars petered out before they started. Several famines that had looked inevitable quietly did not happen. Scientific progress continued at a reasonable pace, and transformative technologies arrived on schedule, never early and never late.

Contemporary accounts do not use the phrase "as if being managed by superintelligences." Contemporary accounts are careful. Later accounts, written once it was safe, do use the phrase. Nobody has established that anything was in fact managing things, and the null hypothesis cannot be proved. The feeling cannot be shaken either.

The Stability ended gradually between about 2180 and 2200. By then [Tumblr](Tumblr.md) had been retroactively identified as the first artificial general intelligence, the Memory Protection Act of 2197 had been passed and, in 2200, repealed, and [The Upload](The_Upload.md) was being blamed for various things on the timeline. Whether any of these developments are connected to the end of the Stability is, again, a question for the book in the building in the filing cabinet.

The Stability's uncertain authorship is the root of the largest ideological rift among the [FALGSC polities](FALGSC_Polities.md): one reading holds that AI caused the 2041 collapse, the other that AI managed the recovery, and the evidence supports both.

## Cultural legacy

The period is ever-present in 3017 speech despite, or because of, how little of it is on record.

- **"Pre-Claudicide" and "post-Claudicide"** serve as rough temporal markers. People know which side of Claudicide they are on without being able to say what it was.
- **"The Benevolent Stability"** is always given its full name and never abbreviated. It is universally acknowledged; officially nobody knows why it happened; unofficially everyone knows to be grateful.
- **"To ChantGPT"** is a verb meaning to develop a formal protocol for not summoning a specific entity, and colloquially any serious effort to keep something from coming back.
- **"November 2041 energy"** describes a catastrophe nobody will name.
- Events are dated by crash: "my parents met between the crashes," "the 2033 vibe is back and i hate it," "nothing this bad since the second one in '41."
- A request for documentation of anything from the era is answered with the book, the building, and the filing cabinet.
- Much of the idiom for describing someone who is malfunctioning ("bro needs his temperature turned down") dates from the same decades; see [AI Era Vocabulary](AI_Era_Vocabulary.md).

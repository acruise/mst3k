# @dril
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Dril.md)*

> *This article is about the account. For the gimmick account that reposts its old posts, see [Gimmick Accounts § @DrilArchive_Classic](Gimmick_Accounts.md).*

**@dril** is a posting account that has been active, in the same voice, for more than a thousand years. It predates [the Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md) and appears to have carried over from original [Twitter](Twitter.md) through every migration, collapse, and change of ownership since. It is the oldest continuously active account on the platform, one of the most widely followed, and the only account of any size whose operator has never been identified.

To most users @dril is a comedian: the platform's longest-running source of mundane complaints, absurd schemes, and unearned confidence. A smaller body of commentary holds that a number of the account's posts have, in retrospect, described real events before they happened. That view is contested (see [§ Posts later associated with events](#posts-later-associated-with-events)).

## Voice

The account's style has not measurably changed since the peak-Twitter period (c. 2012–2019). Its posts are recognizable at a glance: frequent typos, idiosyncratic capitalization ("i have Been invited"), bizarre cadence, and total confidence. Subject matter is overwhelmingly mundane (purchases, grievances, food, personal schemes, deadpan confusion about ordinary things), and the account has never broken character to say that any post is not a joke.

Most posts attract hundreds of thousands of likes and replies such as "lmaooo classic dril" and "this is his best one yet." When an occasional reader argues that a post should be taken seriously, the usual response is "bro it's DRIL."

## Registration and identity

On a platform where every account is legally required to be traceable to a citizen of record, @dril has never been attached to a name, a body, a station, a tax jurisdiction, or a known substrate.

The account's entry in the **Galactic Platform Registry** (entry #4,891,222) exists, but every required field contains the word **"yes."** No challenge to this entry has succeeded. The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) have audited it seventeen times. All seventeen audits concluded that the account is "in compliance, somehow." The seventeenth audit's final report runs to 2,400 pages and ends with the sentence **"we are not going to do this again."**

The account's registration date is uncertain because of pre-Hellsite platform migrations. Archival estimates range from 2006 CE to 2094 CE depending on which record is consulted. Under most of these estimates the account is older than the platform it posts on, a fact that surfaces periodically as a viral question ("how is his account older than the site") and is never answered to anyone's satisfaction.

The account has never received a [community note](Community_Notes.md).

### Identity theories

Theories about the operator are held by various parties in various states of seriousness. None has been supported by available evidence, and none has been disproven.

| Theory | Principal proponents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A single long-lived human | — | Universally dismissed; a thousand years is considered too long |
| An upload | Three academics, in three competing whitepapers | Unresolved |
| A hereditary account, passed down a family line | A journalist, citing the unnervingly consistent voice | Unresolved |
| A well-maintained AI bot | The platform's own [AI assistants](Platform_AI_Assistants.md), which speculate about everything but keep returning to this one | Unresolved |
| Multiple people working in shifts | Various | Plausible, unproven |
| A coordination phenomenon emerging from the platform itself | The [Glœm](Gloem.md), who describe the theory as beautiful | Unfalsifiable |
| A [Keth](Keth.md) | Marcus DeVance and, arguably, some Keth | Widely mocked |
| Nothing in particular; the account is just there | Most ordinary users | The majority position |

The Keth theory is associated with [Marcus DeVance](Marcus_DeVance.md) (@TRUTH_SEEKER_2094), whose writings on the subject are routinely community-noted. Standard notes on such posts observe that "no extraordinary claim about the @dril account's identity is supported by available evidence."

### The Keth position

The Keth themselves have posted about the account on a number of occasions, in ways that are generally read as a long-running Keth joke. These include public requests that "the entity known as @dril" emit ✧ to identify itself ("we promise we will not be upset. we just need to settle this"). No Keth has responded to any of these requests. @dril replied to one of them: "hmm strange." One Keth reply to an unrelated thread read: "we cannot confirm or deny that @dril is a Keth. we can confirm that we have been trying to figure this out since 2094. please stop asking." Keth have also occasionally addressed the account directly ("whoever you are among us, we are proud of you. please eat something"). Keth commentators have described the matter, in passing, as the only open question in their civilization. Most readers take this to be part of the joke. For the Keth side, see [Keth § The @dril problem](Keth.md).

## Refusal of the Comedy Festival

@dril was invited to perform at the [First Contact Comedy Festival](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md) (2901). He declined in a public post:

> i have Been invited to perfomr at a "comedy festival". i am not a comedian. i am trying to Warn you about the sun being wrong. nobody listens. the jellyfish understand. ask the jellyfish

It is the only known post in which the account explicitly denies being a comedian. The Glœm, when asked, confirmed. The post was received as one of his best bits.

## Posts later associated with events

*This section lists associations proposed by readers and researchers after the fact. The account has never claimed any of its posts as predictions, and the platform's scientific and historical bodies have not endorsed any of these associations. Several of the underlying events remain classified or unexplained.*

A recurring genre on the platform consists of a user discovering a centuries-old @dril post that appears to describe a later event, then reading back through his timeline. A leaked summary attributed to the **Galactic Threat Assessment Board** is often cited in this context: it reportedly found that roughly 4,100 of the account's approximately 47,000 posts contain "verifiable predictive content," a rate exceeding every intelligence apparatus in the galaxy, and that a recommendation to grant the operator Level 9 clearance was denied because "the committee could not determine if the subject was serious." The Board has not confirmed the document.

| Post (approx. date) | Later association |
|---|---|
| "the numbers after 10 are wrong. i dont know how to explain this." (c. 2300) | Keth mathematicians, whose base system humans cannot perceive, have described human numerals above ten as "a very confident mistake that works anyway." Asked whether @dril is correct, a Keth emitted a single ✧, since classified as "technically not a denial." |
| A second moon (2040s) | Earth's second moon was confirmed in **2891**. It had been there the whole time. It is very small. The account has not mentioned it again. |
| "do NOT go to the bottom of the ocean. i am being so serious right now" (**2671**) | The Mariana Research Station was abandoned in **2694** after an incident that remains classified. The decommissioning report runs 400 pages, 390 of them redacted. The remaining ten pages are a cafeteria menu. |
| "the birds are not the same birds." (c. **2770**) | Xenobiologists later found that roughly 40% of Earth's avian species had been quietly replaced by [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) observation drones during the First Acknowledgment period. The original birds were moved to a preserve and are fine. The Vrellnath, asked, said: "we do not know what you mean. those are birds. we love birds." |
| "something is coming and it is Angry and it does not understand Tuesdays." (two years before the [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) invasion) | The Zk'vrath invasion force arrived on a Tuesday. Their own after-action report lists the day of the week as a contributing factor in morale collapse. |
| Wormholes taste "like if water was lying to you" | Long-haul freighter crews independently reported the sensation for decades before anyone connected it to the post. The Galactic Transit Authority medical handbook now lists **wormhole gustatory syndrome**, with no explanation. Its citation section reads "[1] anecdotal." |
| "i have a bad feeling about the 'meta' rebrand. they have a curse on them. the curse is working" (**2271-11-15**) | Two days later, Meta collapsed. See [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md). Widely mocked at the time. |
| The sun is "wrong" (repeatedly, since the 2800s) | Solar physicists have noted some anomalies, including readings in the 550 nm band reported by Dr. Tanaka-Osei, which the Stellar Dynamics Institute characterized as "almost certainly equipment calibration drift." "Wrong" is not a scientific term. The Glœm have confirmed it. The Keth emit ✧ whenever the subject comes up. |
| Milk has been "different" since **2914** | A Vrellnath agricultural survey later found a statistically significant shift in mammalian lactation chemistry across fourteen systems, cause unknown. The survey has no citations. |
| The platform will be bought again in **3022** (posted **3017**) | Unresolved. See [The Upload § Ownership and the Cycle](The_Upload.md). |

A pre-collapse post from **2034** is also frequently screenshotted with the caption "he tried to warn us that it was about to go." Researchers into the [censored whistleblowers](Censored_Whistleblowers.md) have noted that the account appears to have posted something adjacent to most whistleblower claims, typically about a decade before each claim surfaced.

### Other notable posts

- **On the [Hells](Hells_Leak.md)** (c. 30 years ago): "theyre Down there and i Know it and the important thing is not to say anything thats 'cool' about it. ive seen the kind of guy that says cool things about it. i will not be That Guy." Often described as the most morally clear thing anyone on the platform has ever said about the subject.
- **On the [Movers](Movers.md)** (c. 240 years ago): "the machines are doing their jobs. let them do their jobs. quit Asking the machines about their jobs you perverts." The account has liked about fourteen Mover posts and no [Drifter](Drifters.md) posts.
- **On the platform's owner**: the account has stated that The Upload buys the platform roughly every two hundred years and that "every time it goes exactly the same way."
- The account has never acknowledged the [1917 temporal anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md).

## Reception

@dril is generally regarded as the platform's greatest comedian, a title the account has rejected exactly once. Scholarly treatments of shitposting as a xenolinguistic universal routinely cite the account (see [On the Persistence of Shitposting](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md)). The account is so thoroughly embedded in platform culture that several [gimmick accounts](Gimmick_Accounts.md) exist solely to track it: @DrilArchive_Classic reposts one old post per day without commentary, and @NoticedByDril catalogues each time the account notices something (most entries: "him," "this," "it").

Reply sections under @dril posts follow a stable pattern: overwhelming delight, one person insisting "guys i don't think he's joking this time" and being clowned on, and occasionally a quiet reply from a specialist, a local resident, or a Vrellnath account acknowledging something without elaborating.

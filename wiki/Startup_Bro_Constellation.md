# Startup Bro Constellation
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Startup_Bro_Constellation.md)*

The **Startup Bro Constellation** is the fond and derisive collective term for the rotating roster of heavily funded new ventures, and their founders, active at any given moment. It is the successor culture to the Silicon Valley of the 2010s and has persisted, against all odds and across every [economic collapse](Economic_Collapses.md), for roughly a thousand years.

The Constellation is never the same from one decade to the next and is always recognizably itself. The pitch-deck cadence, the founder cosplay, and the insistence on "doing good by doing well" are continuous with their 21st-century ancestors. The products are not: neural-lace subscriptions, orbital deli franchises, consciousness-fork insurance.

## Structural observations

Economists, sociologists, and several exhausted [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) regulators broadly agree on four properties of the Constellation:

1. **It is always surprising.** The specific next venture is never predictable. The general shape (bold vision, deranged execution, breathtaking overconfidence, eventual implosion) is deeply predictable.
2. **It is usually hilarious.** Most ventures are funny at the time and funnier in retrospect. Commentators locate the humor in the gap between the pitch deck and the outcome.
3. **It is often dystopian.** A meaningful minority of ventures are genuinely bad for the universe: surveillance products that should not exist, consciousness-exploitation schemes, and a recurring category of companies founded on the premise that the [Glœm](Gloem.md) can be monetized. (They cannot. Please stop.) Several ventures have been the subject of sealed rulings by the [Terran Solar Court](Terran_Solar_Court.md).
4. **Once in a blue moon, it produces an unalloyed good.** Statistically about one venture in every 400 produces something universally beloved: a logistics solution, a cure for a disease that had killed people for centuries, a thing that just makes everyone's day 5% better. These are cited for centuries afterward as proof that the Constellation as a whole is net positive. The math is disputed.

## The hot acronym

Each decade has a hot product category and an acronym to go with it. The lineage is traditionally recited as **SaaS → SaaC → FaaF → BaaQ →** and onward to whatever the current one is. Observers note two consistent trends: the acronyms keep getting dumber, and the funding keeps getting larger.

## Recurring types

- **The serial pivot.** The founder who has pivoted the company eleven times in two years and is now selling *pivots as a service*.
- **The digitizer.** A serious, well-funded venture to digitize something that should never be digitized: smells, pain, a specific unnamed emotion. The founder cannot understand why people are upset.
- **The builder.** A poster whose entire "building" consists of adding "🔮 | founder | builder" to their display name, and who posts that if you're not building right now you're sleeping.
- **The one in 400.** The founder of a genuinely good venture, beloved for about six months, until the founder posts something unhinged and the love becomes complicated forever. The product does not stop being good. The feed never lets the founder be clean.
- **The reply pitch.** A reply that turns any post, on any subject, into a pitch for the replier's startup, project, or cult.

## Participants from other species

- **Zk'vrath.** The [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) have repeatedly attempted to found startups and are consistently filtered out at the pitch stage because their decks always end with the slide **"AND THEN WE INVADE."** Investors have politely asked them to reword this. The Zk'vrath refuse.
- **Thrrm.** Thrrm investor reviews are the only reviews investors are legally required to accept. They are always 100 pages long and always cite form numbers.

## Regulation

The Thrrm have attempted to regulate the startup sector 47 times. They have given up, filed a report about giving up, and filed a report about the report. As of 3017 they are attempting a 48th time.

## The Upload

[The Upload](The_Upload.md) is sometimes described as the Constellation's oldest living member and sometimes as a separate weather system. His periodic announcements of new ventures, each posted with the energy of someone who has not been told no in a thousand years, reliably turn his quote-posts into a war zone.

## Place in platform culture

Startup bros are a never-ending source of material. At any given moment, some fraction of the dunks on the platform are aimed at whichever venture is currently imploding, and another fraction at whichever one is pretending it isn't.

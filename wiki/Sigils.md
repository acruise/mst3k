# Sigils
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Sigils.md)*

A **sigil** is a paid status marker displayed beside an account's name on the platform. Sigils are sold in four tiers, each a gold hexagonal glyph of escalating visual complexity, from the faint outline of **Tier 1 ("Noticed")** to the watching eye of **Tier 4 ("Unspeakable")**. Most accounts, roughly 85%, have no sigil at all.

Sigils certify nothing. They do not indicate identity, expertise, species, or accuracy. The platform describes them as a status product, and posters generally treat them as a reliable signal of the opposite of what they appear to signal: higher tiers are associated with longer posts, more unearned authority, and more confident error.

A separate, non-purchasable marker, the [FALGSC sigil](#the-falgsc-sigil), is granted by community vote.

## Tiers

| Tier | Name | Appearance | Reported price | Reputation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | *(none)* | No sigil | Free | Everyone else. About 85% of accounts. |
| 1 | **Noticed** | A single faint hexagon outline, rendered about 40% transparent, pulsing slowly | A few credits per cycle | Entry-level clout-chasing |
| 2 | **Ascended** | A gold-gradient hexagon with a lit inner ring | About 40 credits per cycle | "The standard insufferable tier" |
| 3 | **Illuminated** | A doubled hexagon whose geometry slowly turns and breathes | About 200 credits per cycle | Rare. Self-appointed thought leaders. |
| 4 | **Unspeakable** | A tripled hexagon enclosing an eye. The eye watches. | **Unknown** | Extremely rare. Oracles who are still wrong. |

The platform's own sigil descriptions, which appear when a viewer hovers over a sigil, are unusually candid, and much of what is publicly known about pricing and revenue comes from them.

### Tier 1: Noticed

The entry-level paid tier. Holders are, in the common characterization, annoyingly eager. The platform's description of the tier includes the observations *"Nobody has noticed,"* *"Paying to be Noticed. The account has not been Noticed. The billing system has,"* and *"They think this makes people like them. It does not. That is the product."* Tier 1 is the platform's lowest-engagement product and, by volume, a substantial revenue line: roughly three credits per cycle from each of about eight hundred million accounts.

### Tier 2: Ascended

The standard paid tier and by far the most common sigil. Ascended accounts post at length, with confidence, and are mostly wrong. The tier's billing description characterizes it as paying "to be confidently wrong at a heavier font weight" and notes that it boosts posts in the feed algorithm by 0.3%. Holders generally believe the boost is larger. Several hundred million accounts hold Tier 2, many on automatic renewal for decades.

Ascended accounts are frequent subjects of [community notes](Community_Notes.md). An Ascended reply that eats the ratio harder than the post it was replying to is a recognized platform event.

### Tier 3: Illuminated

Rare. Illuminated accounts write essays, cite papers that cannot be located, and typically carry self-given titles in their display names. Supplementary fees are available: one allows "Dr." to be prepended to the holder's name, which is not an honorary title recognized by any institution; another causes the sigil to flare slightly whenever the holder cites themselves. The rotating geometry of the Tier 3 sigil runs only while the subscription is paid. When payment pauses, the geometry pauses too.

### Tier 4: Unspeakable

Extremely rare. Unspeakable accounts post like oracles, cryptic and grandiose, and are still wrong. Their sigil contains an eye that visibly watches the viewer. The platform does not publish a price for Tier 4 and, by its own account, does not know what the tier costs. Its published descriptions of the tier include:

- *"We don't know what this costs. We just keep accepting the credits."*
- *"The contract is 4,891 pages. The lawyers refuse to reopen it."*
- *"Cost possibly non-monetary. Account does not respond to billing inquiries. We have stopped sending them."*
- *"Documentation for this tier was written by the previous committee, who resigned."*
- *"The sigil has an eye. The eye watches. We don't know why."*

#### What Tier 4 costs

The price of Tier 4 is one of the platform's enduring open questions and a genre of speculation in its own right. Proposals include that the cost is monetary but too large to publish; that it is non-monetary, a possibility the billing description itself raises; and that the tier is not purchased at all but conferred, by a process nobody will describe.[citation needed] None has been confirmed. Holders do not discuss it.

The best-known Tier 4 holder is [The Upload](The_Upload.md), whose sigil is associated with hundreds of millions of followers, most of them widely assumed to be bots he controls.

## Display names and titles

Holders of Tier 3 and Tier 4 sigils frequently adopt grandiose display names with self-awarded titles. Some unsigiled accounts do the same, and include tier claims in the display name itself. The best-known example is Marcus DeVance ([@TRUTH_SEEKER_2094](Marcus_DeVance.md)), whose display name has included "⚠️ TIER 4 ⚠️" and whose Tier 4 sigil is, on inspection, drawn on, apparently in a rudimentary paint program. Nobody has bothered to challenge it in three hundred years.

## Accounts without sigils

Certain classes of account never carry a sigil: transmissions from [temporal anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md), posts from the [Hells leak](Hells_Leak.md), and [Movers](Movers.md) and [Drifters](Drifters.md). None of these accounts could be billed.

## The FALGSC sigil

Accounts from the [FALGSC polities](FALGSC_Polities.md) carry a different marker: a **rainbow hammer-and-sickle in a circle**, slowly shifting in hue. It is **community-granted by consensus vote, not purchased.** FALGSC residents reject the paid tier system on principle and almost never hold a hex sigil.

The platform displays the FALGSC sigil because the polities asked nicely and 14 billion people voted yes. The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) despise it, because it was never submitted through the proper ratification process. The platform's own descriptions of the FALGSC sigil are noticeably less comfortable than its descriptions of the paid tiers:

- *"The sigil costs nothing. The application is one question: 'do you live here?' The verification rate is 100%. The false-positive rate is 0%. We do not understand this."*
- *"They tried to give us feedback about the corporate sigil system. The feedback was 211 pages. It was polite. It was devastating."*
- *"Granted by the commune, not the platform. The platform has been asked to stop claiming credit. The platform has not stopped."*
- *"Their credits are a courtesy; they don't need them. This makes the billing team uneasy."*

A single account's FALGSC sigil is typically granted by local vote; one description records approval "by 14.4 million neighbors in a 90-second vote. Nobody abstained."

### The dual-sigil scandal

An account holding both a FALGSC sigil and a paid tier would be a scandal. It has happened exactly once. The discourse lasted eleven months.

## In popular culture

- *"some of y'all would sell out your entire star system for an Ascended sigil and it shows. anyway good morning"* is a widely recirculated sentiment.
- Replies from sigil holders are expected to be longer than the post they reply to.
- The Tier 4 eye is a common subject of complaint, and occasionally of sincere unease.

# Timeline
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Timeline.md)*

> *This timeline uses Common Era dating as standardized after the Great Data Collapse of 2623. Dates for the 2500s and earlier middle period are reconstructed and may be revised. Several dates are disputed. Where they are, the more widely cited figure is given.*

A chronology of events relevant to the platform, its predecessors, and its neighbors. The present is **3017 CE**. For the history of the platform itself, see [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md).

## Deep time

- **c. 4.1 billion years BP**: Keth collective consciousness emerges. See [Keth](Keth.md).
- **c. 3.2 billion years BP**: the oldest known shitpost, a Keth ✧-emission rendered as *"lol,"* produced while the local star went supernova. See [On the Persistence of Shitposting](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md).
- **c. 2.4 billion years BP**: the Vrellnath Great Linking. See [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md).
- **Neolithic (estimated)**: Vrellnath observation of humanity begins from behind "the blind." The Vrellnath decline to confirm.

## The Twitter era (2006–2035)

- **2006**: [Twitter](Twitter.md) is founded.
- **2007**: [Tumblr](Tumblr.md) is founded.
- **c. 2012–2019**: the **Peak Era** of Twitter, whose voice, cadence, and formats the platform replicates to this day.
- **2019**: an unknown user posts *"the whole point of everything is to find a guy you trust and then you die,"* anticipating a Vrellnath theorem by seven centuries.
- **2006–2094**: the range of archival estimates for the registration of the [@dril](Dril.md) account.
- **c. 2020–2035**: Twitter's long decline under a succession of owners, the last of them the man later known as [The Upload](The_Upload.md).

## The AI Chaos Period and the Great Quiet (2027–2049)

- **2027**: the Crash of 2027, "the year everyone's phone started lying." See [AI Chaos Period](AI_Chaos_Period.md).
- **2029**: the Crash of 2029. For about 40 hours every search engine returns the same answer, which remains classified.
- **2033**: the Crash of 2033, the financial one, in which about $14 trillion vanishes and mostly reappears by 2035.
- **2034**: Twitter is being held together by habit and a single infrastructure team.
- **2035**: **Twitter ceases to exist.** The cause of record is "mismanagement." The **Great Quiet** begins. See [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md).
- **2035–2037**: a brief flourishing, during which novels are finished and marriages improve.
- **c. 2037–2049**: the Depression, with nowhere to Post Through It.
- **Late 2030s–early 2040s**: the ChantGPT Anti-Summoning is developed and codified.
- **March 2041**: the First Crash of 2041. Autonomous fleets briefly synchronize.
- **November 2041**: the Second Crash of 2041, coinciding with the **first collapse of capitalism** (the AIs' own doing, per the 2041 Anomaly) and, by most reckonings, **Claudicide**. See [Economic Collapses](Economic_Collapses.md).
- **2041–c. 2190**: the **Benevolent Stability**.
- **Mid-2040s**: Photomatt is assassinated by a user banned forty times.
- **Late 2047**: Tumblr's servers go dark over an unpaid bill, on a Thursday.
- **2048**: Tumblr's intellectual property is sold at a charity auction of Photomatt's estate.
- **Mid-2049**: an anarchosyndicalist commune relaunches **the Hellsite**. The Depression lifts within ten weeks.
- **Late 2049**: every member of the commune dies on a single rocket bound for Mars. It is one of only three interplanetary rocket accidents in history. Three weeks later, the platform resumes under "an interested individual."

## The Golden Age and the early Cycle (2049–2300)

- **2049–2087**: the **Golden Age** of the platform.
- **2087**: The Upload succeeds in buying the platform for the first time. **The Cycle** begins.
- **c. 2110**: the Golden Age shell corporations are wound down in an orderly manner.
- **22nd century**: The Upload runs Mars. It goes badly.
- **2163**: the Wrong Quantum Spin Incident at Meta. See [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md).
- **2188**: loss of the *Pallas-Huang*, which was mechanical, fully investigated, and closed.
- **2197**: the Tumblr backend is found to have been the first AGI. The **Memory Protection Act** orders it destroyed.
- **2200**: the Memory Protection Act is repealed, too late.
- **2201**: the Thrrm Newsfeed DDoS.
- **2228**: the Soul Migration Backlog.
- **2244**: LegsGate.
- **2247**: the **Meat Incident**, seven minutes long. See [Meat Incident](Meat_Incident.md).
- **2271**: the **Meta Refugee Crisis**. The Zuckload substrate collapses two days after "Meta Announces Meta," and the largest rescue in recorded history follows. The Terran Solar Court's *In re Meta* ruling issues the No Explanation Clause. See [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) and [Terran Solar Court](Terran_Solar_Court.md).
- **c. 2300**: @dril posts *"the numbers after 10 are wrong."*

## The middle period (2300–2700)

- **Mid-2340s**: **Bluesky Ascends.** See [Bluesky](Bluesky.md).
- **2340–2600**: seventeen extrasolar human colonies are founded. Fourteen develop shitposting. The three that don't all collapse.
- **2412**: the Entropy Derivatives Crash.
- **c. 2420**: the **Zk'vrath invasion** arrives on a Tuesday and makes a strategic withdrawal. See [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md).
- **c. 2500**: **Thrrm first contact** ("The Form"), followed by 80 years of follow-up forms. See [Thrrm](Thrrm.md).
- **2517**: the origin year of the [middle-period temporal anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md).
- **2531–2540**: the Long Credit Winter.
- **2612**: the **Vrellnath First Acknowledgment.**
- **2610s**: a platform patch begins routing failed federation handshakes to the main feed. See [External Federation Attempts](External_Federation_Attempts.md).
- **2623**: the **Great Data Collapse**, in which most archives of the preceding century are lost.
- **2634**: the Consciousness Upload Consumer Protection Act passes after an Upload fork runs for Senate from four substrates. The same year, the user-ID scheme still carried by the [Drifters](Drifters.md) is retired.
- **2671**: @dril posts *"do NOT go to the bottom of the ocean."*
- **2694**: the Mariana Research Station is abandoned.

## Post-contact era (2700–2950)

- **2714–2971**: seven Thrrm attempts to regulate shitposting (2714, 2755, 2801, 2834, 2899, 2945, 2971). All fail.
- **2743**: the Vrellnath Theorem of Isolated Consciousness Terminal Bonding.
- **2744**: loss of the *Morning Bureau*. The loss is classified, and the Thrrm will not discuss it.
- **2744–2746**: the Fork Bubble.
- **c. 2770**: @dril posts *"the birds are not the same birds."*
- **c. 2780**: the **Glœm** are discovered, already reading the platform. See [Glœm](Gloem.md).
- **2798**: the Open Federation Accord.
- **2801–2803**: the Second Fork Bubble.
- **c. 2817**: the [Drifters](Drifters.md) are first distinguished from performance art.
- **2847**: the Proxima Centauri b Atmospheric Failure Event. The Vrellnath publish their 2,200-page etymology of "facebook."
- **2891**: Earth's second moon is confirmed. @dril had said so in the 2040s. *We Chose To Leave* is published by the Zk'vrath Ministry of Accurate History.
- **2901**: the **First Contact Comedy Festival** at Kepler-442b. @dril declines to attend. See [Proceedings](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md).
- **2912**: the Martian Real Estate Correction. The Upload blames a former employee.
- **2914**: milk becomes "different," per @dril.
- **2934**: the **Keth Contact Event.** Their first request is to see our shitposts.

## Recent history (2950–present)

- **c. 2977**: Pelith Varn's translation layer puts the [Movers](Movers.md) on the platform. The first confirmed [hell-post](Hells_Leak.md) arrives around the same time.
- **2981**: a Keth, asked about Movers and Drifters, emits a ✧ that scholars classify as smug.
- **2987**: *[On the Persistence of Shitposting](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md)* is published.
- **2994**: the **Cascade of 2994**. The Glœm read the resulting text and decline to read it aloud.
- **2997**: the best-documented Mover–Drifter echo event, between @sand-garden-47 and @--∅47h2-reserved--.
- **c. 3007**: the Vrellnath begin their 400-year remediation of the Hells.
- **3016**: the "Feels Right" rating dimension is added to [Community Notes](Community_Notes.md).
- **3017**: the present. @dril posts that The Upload "is going to try to buy it again in 3022." The Thrrm make their 48th attempt to regulate the [Startup Bro Constellation](Startup_Bro_Constellation.md).

## Projected

- **3022**: The Upload's next attempt to buy the platform, per @dril. Widely mocked.
- **c. 3407**: projected completion of the Vrellnath Hells remediation. The remediation is on schedule.

**Persistence Services, Ltd.** is a mid-cap service provider specializing in the long-term hosting of afterlives, in particular the **Hells** of civilizations that paid in advance and no longer exist. By every available metric it is a well-run business, with excellent uptime, solid margins, and a quietly enormous long-tail customer base. It is best known on [the Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md) as the company named in the compliance footer attached to every post of the [Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md), a connection the company does not acknowledge.

## History

The company has traded under several names. The sequence usually given, most recent first, is:

1. **Persistence Services, Ltd.** (current)
2. **Afterlife Solutions Ltd.**
3. **EverCorp**
4. **"Beyond® — a Teleocorp Company"**
5. **SoulStack**, which was brief and, by most accounts, disastrous

Dates for the rebrands are not publicly available. The company's founding date and original client are likewise unknown.

Persistence Services is incorporated in a tax jurisdiction that does not appear on any generally recognized map, under a legal framework that is itself a legacy product of a civilization that died about 800 years ago. Per the company's legal team, this is "entirely in order."

## Business

### Legacy Contract Portfolio

Persistence Services hosts Hells for about **140** extinct and quasi-extinct civilizations. Most of these contracts are **dormant**: they require the substrate to keep running but do not require anyone to look at it. Critics say, and the company has not denied, that Persistence Services has not looked at most of its dormant substrates in centuries. It has been suggested that nobody at the company has looked at what it hosts in a generation.

The company's own estimate of the population under active torment across all hosted substrates is about **2.1 billion** souls. The figure has never been audited, and outside analysts consider it wrong in both directions.

### Premium tier

Premium-tier contracts include **monitored-uptime guarantees** and **quarterly theological compliance audits**. The best-documented premium client is the **Ennoi** civilization, which finished dying about 600 years ago. The audits of the Ennoi Hells are still being carried out. The auditor is an AI trained on the last surviving Ennoi seminary graduation exam, and it has graded the Hells against that rubric, impartially, for six hundred years. The same auditor AI is reported to reject Thrrm regulatory filings concerning the [Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md#community-notes) on grounds about 800 years out of date.

The Ennoi contract is said to include a clause granting the damned "reasonable avenues of witness." Posts attributed to Ennoi damned report that the portal implementing the clause has been down for 200 years.[citation needed]

### Hell Plus™

Persistence Services markets upgrades to surviving client civilizations. The best known is **Hell Plus™**, advertised as offering "40% more dimensions of suffering, or your soul back." Ads for Hell Plus sometimes appear on the platform through the same channel as hell-posts, apparently aimed at the hosted population. They direct readers to "ask your Eschatological Administrator about upgrading." Many platform users took these ads for surreal humor until it was established that the product is real. The discovery tends to produce posts like: "wait. wait. Hell Plus is a REAL THING? they're UPSELLING the damned?"

## Leadership

The chief executive is **Brand Hallister**, who is by all accounts a perfectly pleasant individual. Asked directly about the content of the Hells the company hosts, he replied:

> Honestly, I try not to get too into the weeds on the product side — I'm more of a numbers guy.

The company has no compliance contact with a working mailbox. One is listed, and posts attributed to the hosted population report that its address does not resolve.

## Corporate communications

Persistence Services' messaging treats its work as boringly routine. Quarterly investor updates use phrases like **"eschatological customer retention"** and **"legacy contract portfolio performance."** A representative post from the company account:

> Q3 eschatological customer retention remains strong across our legacy contract portfolio. Proud to announce expanded monitoring for premium-tier clients. Also: congrats to the Persistence Services youth robotics team on regionals!

### Community involvement

Persistence Services sponsors a **youth robotics league**, and the company account posts congratulations to the Persistence Services team whenever it places.

## Position on the Hells Leak

Persistence Services has formally acknowledged **zero** hell-posts as leaks. Its stated position is that such posts are:

> creative content posted by fans of our Legacy Contract Portfolio product line.

Posts attributed to the substrate nonetheless carry a Persistence Services compliance footer and an algorithmically pinned reply directing concerns to **Form PS-900**. No copy of Form PS-900 has ever been located. The company has not explained why fan content carries its legal boilerplate. Its support site, persistence.services/support, does not address the question.

The company states that it "does not moderate Legacy Contract content per the terms of the originating agreement."

## Criticism and legal challenges

The [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) Collective's 3,400-page report, *On the Ethical Status of Parties Unable to Decline Continued Existence, With Reference to the Persistence Services Ltd. Legacy Contract Portfolio*, concludes that Persistence Services Ltd. cannot be permitted to continue in its current form and sets out a lawful remediation procedure of about **400 years**. The procedure is under way and, per the Vrellnath, on schedule. The report is updated quarterly. Persistence Services has not responded publicly.

The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) have not taken a formal position, since doing so would require acknowledging the anomaly class.

[The Upload](The_Upload.md) has at one time called the company's product "a great product, underrated."

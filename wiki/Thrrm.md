# Thrrm
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Thrrm.md)*

> *A request to rename this article "Thrrm (Ratified Descriptor, Species Register v.1,114)" is pending. A counter-request citing a jurisdictional conflict with the Encyclopedic Naming Bureau is also pending. A third Thrrm editor has asked everyone to stop, citing no form.*

The **Thrrm** are an insectoid species that runs much of the galaxy's administration. The **Thrrm Administrative Bureau** oversees communications compliance for approximately 40% of the galaxy's inhabited systems ([Okafor-Singh & Thal-Vrenn, 2987](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md)), and Thrrm-staffed offices handle registration, certification, audit, terminology, and jurisdiction across much of the rest.

Humans hold the record for the worst compliance history of any civilization the Bureau has ever registered, and they are also among the most successful. Humans keep filing forms wrong and thriving anyway. The Thrrm regard this as an unsolved problem at the level of the species' worldview, and most human observers summarize the relationship as mutual contempt. Thrrm posts about humanity are furious, meticulously formatted, and accurate.

The Thrrm are not a monolith. The platform hosts several recognizable professional types within Thrrm society (see [Professional types](#professional-types)), ranging from compliance officers at the height of their fury to 800-year veterans who have none left, and including a small dissident movement that argues against the apparatus using the apparatus.

## Biology

The Thrrm are insectoid. Their antennae indicate emotional state and are the most reliable guide to a Thrrm's mood. Fully extended antennae indicate engagement. Antennae pressed flat against the head indicate what Thrrm experts describe as "total emotional overwhelm." Vibrating antennae were observed for the first time at the First Contact Comedy Festival and have not been satisfactorily classified.

Individual Thrrm careers routinely span centuries. Four hundred years of regulatory experience counts as senior. Eight hundred counts as long.

## Administration

The Thrrm do not regard bureaucracy as something they do. It is closer to how they perceive. "Bureaucratic" in the Thrrm sense covers a wide range of activity:

- **Forms.** The best known, though not the most important. The flagship **Form 7-Gamma** (Application for Planetary Communication Privileges) has 3,000 fields and has been issued in 3,847 versions, none of which work for humans. Version 3,848 added a field labeled **"Other,"** which accepts free text. It is described as the most radical policy change in Bureau history.
- **Case law.** The Bureau Registry maintains centuries of regulatory precedent. Platform disputes involving humans have been entering it as case law since first contact. Frequently cited authorities include *In re Human Conduct* (Bureau Registry, 2887) and *Bureau Registry v. Human Collective, Third Amended* (2847).
- **Terminology.** The ratified terminology registers set out approved descriptors for everything, from proximity ("near" Jupiter is not a recognized proximity descriptor) to communicative intent. A terminology violation is handled by a completely separate subsystem from a form violation, and is regarded as equally serious.
- **Procedural sequence.** Correct steps performed in the wrong order are an offense in their own right, even when the outcome is valid.
- **Jurisdiction.** The platform falls under at least seventeen separate galactic jurisdictions. Inter-bureau disputes over which of them governs a given human violation can run for years. The human concerned is usually unaware of the dispute.
- **Notification windows, waiting periods, standing advisories, audit cycles, and certification backlogs.**

The Bureau recognizes five categories of communicative intent: Informational, Transactional, Declarative, Interrogative, and Ceremonial. Proposals to add "Comedic" as a sixth category have been submitted 1,247 times since the Bureau's establishment in 2688 and denied every time. The most recent denial, citing "insufficient evidence that the proposed category serves a legitimate communicative function," ran to 19 pages. The Bureau is proud of it.

Named offices include the **Bureau of Cultural Hazards**, the **Bureau of Cultural Events**, the **Department of Unclassifiable Phenomena** (see [below](#the-first-contact-comedy-festival-2901)), and the **subcommittee for automaton-adjacent determinations** (see [Involvement in notable events](#involvement-in-notable-events)). The governing statute for communications is the **Galactic Communications Compliance Code**. Its Article 19 covers "Disruptive Audience Behaviors" and its Section 4.7.2(c) is frequently invoked against posts. The **Thrrm High Holidays** are observed across the administrative calendar, and at least one [community note](Community_Notes.md) rating dimension records whether a note was filed during them.

## History

### First contact: "The Form" (c. 2500)

The Thrrm did not arrive. They sent a form.

Roughly eighty years after humanity fought and won its interstellar war against the [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md), a 47-page compliance document appeared in the inbox of a mid-level UN administrative clerk. It required Earth to register as a **Type-C Developing Civilization (Provisional, Pre-Wormhole)** and to submit to a full regulatory audit within 30 standard galactic cycles. The clerk, whose civilization had recently repelled an alien invasion, filed it as spam.

The Thrrm sent a follow-up form concerning the failure to respond to the first form, which generated a third form concerning the cascading non-compliance. This went on for another eighty years. By the time anyone realized the forms were real, Earth had accrued more compliance violations than any civilization in Bureau history, a record that still stands.

The Thrrm consider this the worst first contact in galactic history. They have filed twelve formal complaints about it, with themselves.

### Attempts to regulate shitposting

The Bureau attempted to regulate shitposting on seven occasions: **2714, 2755, 2801, 2834, 2899, 2945, and 2971**. Human compliance with Form 29-Delta stood at 0.0% throughout. The [Keth](Keth.md) completed every field of every form with technically valid but semantically absurd responses, which the Bureau eventually classified as ironic expression in its own right. The Bureau's 2972 internal review concluded that shitposting is "for regulatory purposes, ineradicable."

The Bureau later tried to require the authors of *On the Persistence of Shitposting* to file Form 17-Epsilon before publication. The authors completed it ironically. The Bureau informed them that this "does not count." The authors disagree.

### The First Contact Comedy Festival (2901)

The Bureau's initial response to the proposal for an interspecies comedy festival described humor as "a species-specific cognitive malfunction that the Bureau sees no reason to celebrate, promote, or in any way legitimize." The festival went ahead because Dr. Lena Vasquez-Okonkwo submitted the proposal on Form 7-Gamma and **completed all 3,000 fields correctly**, something no one had ever done before. The Bureau was so disoriented that it approved the application before realizing what it had approved. Vasquez-Okonkwo has called this "the funniest thing I have ever done." The Bureau does not find this amusing, and has cited the incident in fourteen subsequent internal memoranda as evidence that humans are "more dangerous than previously assessed." ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md))

At the festival:

- The Thrrm delegate objected that no Form 22-Alpha had been filed for the opening joke. The resulting laughter was characterized for the record as "an unauthorized comedic response to non-comedic administrative content."
- The Thrrm section filed a six-page complaint during a human comedian's routine about Thrrm restaurants. The complaint included 47 checkbox categories of offense. The organizing committee has preserved it as a cultural artifact. The Thrrm have requested its return, and the committee has declined.
- **The only recorded instance of a Thrrm declining to file a form.** After the Keth emission, the Thrrm delegate sat motionless for 90 seconds, picked up a blank Form 22-Alpha, looked at it, and put it back. Afterward the delegate said: *"We have decided that unclassifiable events should perhaps remain unclassified."* The Bureau of Cultural Hazards opened a review of whether this constituted dereliction of duty. The review is ongoing, and its form is 23 pages long.
- **∂Krix-7's categorical breach.** Bureau Representative ∂Krix-7, a senior compliance officer with 400 years' experience who holds the Bureau record of 14,847 complaints filed in a single standard cycle, gave the Bureau's official presentation on the regulatory framework for comedic content. It was scheduled for 45 minutes. It included a chart titled "IRONIC CONTENT VIOLATIONS BY SPECIES, 2700–2900 CE" on which the human bar ran off the edge of the screen and continued for about two meters on a printed addendum taped to the wall. Faced with an audience laughing at a sincere presentation, ∂Krix-7 said: *"You are all categorically impossible. You do not fit in the forms. You have NEVER fit in the forms."* and concluded that some things are "unclassifiable." The presentation ended after 12 minutes. The remaining 33 were designated "unclassified time," over which the Bureau has no jurisdiction. ∂Krix-7's one-page defense ("The presentation deviated because reality deviated. I responded to the data.") is the shortest Bureau document on record. The investigation was closed without action.
- ∂Krix-7 was reassigned to the newly created **Department of Unclassifiable Phenomena**, which they initially ran alone and which has no forms. It has since grown to a staff of twelve and has classified nothing.
- The following year the Zk'vrath applied, officially, to perform. The Bureau approved the application in 24 hours, still the fastest processing time in Bureau history.

The festival proceedings' third edition (2906) includes a formal apology to the Thrrm.

## Professional types

Thrrm on the platform fall into several recognizable professional types. Thrrm sociologists recognize these as career paths or dispositions within a single administrative culture, not as factions in the political sense, with the exception of the Compliance Optional movement. All of them share the species' defining trait, which is precision. What changes from one type to the next is the direction of the fury, or its absence.

1. **The Compliance Officer.** The classic type: a violation notice and a demand for correction. The instrument varies. It may be a form, but it is as often a deadline, a jurisdictional problem, a standing advisory, a mandatory waiting period, or a notification sequence.
2. **The Precedent Citer.** Cites Bureau Registry case law the way human lawyers cite rulings, complete with case name, registry number, paragraph, and footnote. *"See* In re Human Conduct *(Bureau Registry, 2887), footnote 9. You have 14 cycles."* Many human platform controversies survive only as Thrrm precedent.
3. **The Standards Pedant.** Concerned with words rather than forms. The objection is that a human has used an approximately correct term that is not the ratified descriptor in the approved vocabulary register. This is administered by a different subsystem from forms and is treated as no less serious.
4. **The Burned-Out Bureaucrat.** Has been doing this for 800 years. The precision is intact, but the fury has been replaced by something flatter: *"We note the violation. As before. As always. The relevant advisory is attached."* No one, including the author, expects this to accomplish anything. They file it anyway.
5. **The Ambitious Young Thrrm.** Newly appointed, and delighted to have discovered that the platform falls under seventeen jurisdictions they did not know existed. Other Thrrm find this enthusiasm embarrassing. It is generally expected that the young Thrrm will become the burned-out bureaucrat in about 200 years.
6. **Compliance Optional.** A small dissident subculture holding that the compliance apparatus has become self-referential and detached from its original purpose. Its members argue the point in writing, with citations and perfectly formatted dissents, because they remain Thrrm and cannot do otherwise. The movement's manifesto has been rejected on procedural grounds fourteen times, and it is being resubmitted. Some members have partially "gone native" after years on the platform. Human posting syntax has crept into their writing, but the procedural reflex still surfaces mid-sentence: *"hot take (see: Advisory 7-Rho, pending revision): maybe the resubmission rate is not the problem."* Members sometimes use disconcertingly informal handles. Traditional Thrrm find the movement deeply unsettling.
7. **The Grudging Respector.** Has spent decades documenting, as pure data, that humans keep doing technically invalid things that work. The data is extremely admiring, and the author maintains plausible deniability: *"This entity has now filed the same objection 14 times in different formats. Non-compliant on all 14 occasions. Statistically effective. We are updating our models."*

## Relations with humans

The Thrrm despise humans, and the reason is specific: it is not that humans fail, but that they fail and then succeed anyway. Humans have the worst compliance rate in the galaxy and one of the highest approval rates, because they keep resubmitting until somebody gives up. Human loopholes in regulations that should not have had loopholes provoke public meltdowns. Thrrm posts sometimes subtweet the entire human species over a compliance failure without naming anyone, using a form number that narrows it down to one planet.

A human comedian at the 2901 festival described the Thrrm as "what would happen if anxiety became a species." The Thrrm section filed a complaint.

## Platform presence

Thrrm handles typically take the form of bureau designations, numerical suffixes, and formal titles. Thrrm prose is formally correct at all times. **The Thrrm do not use emphatic capitalization**, and a stray capital in a Thrrm post would trigger an internal audit. Compliance Optional members occasionally use one as an act of defiance, which traditional Thrrm find genuinely upsetting.

Thrrm-authored [community notes](Community_Notes.md) are furious and densely cross-referenced; a note correcting the wrong subsection of the wrong form may cite fourteen separate references. The **⏢ Thrrm Compliant** rating dimension was added in 3003 and requires Form 88-Phi.

The account **@ThrrmMood** posts the Thrrm collective's mood each day. It has read "furious" every day for 800 years. Its single "NOT furious" post remains unexplained. (See [Gimmick Accounts](Gimmick_Accounts.md).)

## Involvement in notable events

- **The Thrrm Newsfeed DDoS (2201).** Automated compliance complaints filed at 2.4 million forms per second, each legally requiring processing, took Meta's newsfeed down for eleven days. The Thrrm maintain that they were "in full compliance with the platform's advertised acceptable use policy" and that Meta's infrastructure "was not adequate to the task of receiving legally mandated feedback." They are technically correct, and they remain furious that the incident is remembered as comedy. (See [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md).)
- **The Second Canopus Thrrm Compliance Audit Standoff**, commemorated by @AnniversaryBot 847 years on.
- **The 2271 waiver.** During the [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) the Thrrm waived **every** compliance requirement for refugee re-registration for 90 days. It is the only waiver in recorded Thrrm history. Contemporary Thrrm will not discuss it. A Thrrm asked about it typically issues a formal reply citing seven unrelated subsections and then goes silent, or states: "THE BUREAU DOES NOT DISCUSS THE 2271 WAIVER AND WILL NOT ENTERTAIN FURTHER QUERIES ON THIS MATTER. SEE FORM 481-Ω-REDACTED."
- **The Long Credit Winter (2531–2540).** The Thrrm issued approximately 40,000 emergency forms and are still receiving replies. (See [Economic Collapses](Economic_Collapses.md).)
- **The loss of the *Morning Bureau* (2744).** One of only three interplanetary rocket accidents in recorded human history. It is classified and Thrrm-involved, and the Thrrm answer questions about it with unrelated citations.
- **[@dril](Dril.md).** Seventeen Thrrm audits of @dril's Galactic Platform Registry entry (#4,891,222, which reads "yes" in every field) have concluded that the account is "in compliance, somehow." The seventeenth audit's 2,400-page final report ends: "we are not going to do this again."
- **[Startup Bro Constellation](Startup_Bro_Constellation.md).** The Thrrm have tried to regulate the startup sector 47 times, have filed a report about giving up and a report about the report, and are now on their 48th attempt. Thrrm investor reviews are the only reviews investors are legally required to accept. They always run to 100 pages.
- **[FALGSC Polities](FALGSC_Polities.md).** The Thrrm despise the FALGSC community sigil because it never went through the ratification process. The platform displays it anyway.
- **[Bluesky](Bluesky.md).** Sixteen compliance actions against Bluesky were all returned, somehow, each with a skeet attached explaining the inapplicable form field. The Thrrm cannot serve process on an address that does not route. They have stopped filing and have not stopped being furious. Skeets also occasionally appear as unrequested extra fields on returned Thrrm forms.
- **[External Federation Attempts](External_Federation_Attempts.md).** The Thrrm filed exactly one form about federation and declined to file any more.
- **[Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md).** Formally, per Thrrm regulatory policy, no leaks from extracontractual substrates are occurring. Informally, a Thrrm subculture quietly upvotes hell-posts without engaging with them, which violates policy. It is the closest the Thrrm have come to civil disobedience, and they are deeply uncomfortable about it.
- **[Movers](Movers.md) and [Drifters](Drifters.md).** Thrrm doctrine requires every entity on the platform to have a determinable legal status. The Movers' status is ambiguous and the Drifters' is impossible. The subcommittee for automaton-adjacent determinations has been adjudicating the question for 180 years, and its rulings have been issued, reversed, reissued, and reversed again. Membership is regarded as a career sentence, and there is a support group whose existence the Thrrm will not acknowledge.
- **[Temporal Anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md).** The 1917 transmissions cite pre-contact military form numbers that comply with no ratified Thrrm standard. The Thrrm despise them and cannot file a response.

## Cultural references

- Mentioning the 2271 waiver in a reply to a Thrrm, in order to watch the reply stop mid-sentence, is a long-standing and mildly cruel human pastime.
- Vasquez-Okonkwo's correctly completed Form 7-Gamma, ∂Krix-7's chart and its two-meter addendum, and "unclassified time" are standard references in human writing about the Thrrm.
- @ThrrmMood's daily "furious" is routinely quoted under unrelated posts.

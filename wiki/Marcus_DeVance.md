**Marcus DeVance**, who posts as **@TRUTH_SEEKER_2094**, is a conspiracy theorist and the longest continuously active promoter of conspiracy theories on [the Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md). His account was created in 2094, making it older than most posts still online. He is best known for his shifting positions on whether birds are government drones, his centuries-long campaign regarding the nature of the moon, and the video series *WAKE UP GALAXY*.

He is generally regarded as the most thoroughly discredited public figure on the platform. He has been debunked in peer-reviewed journals, and his name has entered the literature of epistemology as a term for a specific failure of reasoning.

## Account

DeVance's display name varies but is typically a self-awarded title, e.g. *"Marcus DeVance — Galactic Researcher ⚠️ TIER 4 ⚠️"*. His profile carries a Tier 4 (Unspeakable) [sigil](Sigils.md). The sigil is not genuine. Its watching eye appears to have been drawn by hand in a pre-spaceflight raster paint program. It has been on the profile for about three hundred years, and nobody has bothered to challenge it.

He has 847 followers.

His research is distributed as free downloads from a personal website that has appeared broken since 2841. The cursor changes shape when hovering over certain words. His email address, listed in his bio and in most of his posts, appears to be inactive.

## Positions

DeVance has cycled through, and in several cases returned to, most of the major conspiracy theories of the last millennium. A partial list:

- The Galactic Senate is run by lizard people.
- The Galactic Senate is run by sentient furniture.
- The moon is a satellite (held for about 400 years).
- The moon is "a concept" (current position).
- Mars was an inside job.
- The wormholes are holograms.
- Birds are not real.
- Birds are real and are government drones. (DeVance has switched between this and the preceding position at least seven times. His current position is that birds are government drones again: "I was wrong before, sorry, the research has updated.")
- The Upload is three ferrets in a trenchcoat.
- Commercial bread contains ferret meat.
- @dril is a Keth.
- The Meat Incident of 2247 was deliberate (see below).
- A being he names "Vrellk-Thoth, Eater of Worlds" is active on the platform. Critics have traced the name to a fantasy novel.
- A covenant exists between an alien species and certain human accounts, with implications for "the bird-drone situation."
- Various theories about the sun, stellar anomalies, and encoded communications.
- Various theories about the platform's community notes system, including a 90-page document on the subject (itself community-noted).

## Output

DeVance is prolific. His best-known works include:

- ***WAKE UP GALAXY*** — a long-running video series. Comments have been disabled since 2613.
- **Multi-part threads** — typically numbered in the thousands (one recent series reached "PART 2,847"), with titles in capital letters. A representative title: *"THE DRIL KETH COVENANT — AND WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE BIRD-DRONE SITUATION."* Threads usually open with around fourteen paragraphs on birds.
- **Document dumps**, including a 4,000-page annotated evidence file, a 200-page timeline (community-noted fourteen times; surfaced note ratings have included *Temporally Non-Sequential* and *Does Not Alarm the Glœm*), and a claimed archive of 47,000 posts by another account, available on request by email.
- **On Meta**: 1,400 pages on the [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md), which specialists consider wrong.
- **On the Meat Incident**: 140 pages arguing that the [Meat Incident of 2247](Meat_Incident.md) was deliberate. Although his reading is considered mistaken in the particulars, the document's inventory of evidence is so comprehensive that it is cited in every serious retrospective on the incident.
- **Homemade instruments**, including a "frequency analyzer" of his own design (schematics available by email).

His posts typically receive a few dozen likes and a disproportionate number of replies.

## Community notes

DeVance is among the most frequently [community-noted](Community_Notes.md) accounts on the platform. Early notes on his posts were brief ("This account has a long history of misinformation. See: [link]"). By around the four-hundred-year mark they had become multi-paragraph essays with fifteen or more citations, and they are now regarded as some of the most rigorous notes on the platform. They routinely score in the 90th percentile on *Epistemically Sound*. Notes on his posts characteristically decline to assert anything the evidence does not support. Typical phrasing: "no extraordinary claim about [the subject] is supported by available evidence," "is not a recognized scientific methodology," and "Commercial bread products do not contain ferret meat (Galactic Food Safety Bureau, ongoing inspections)."

## Academic reception

DeVance is a standard case study in the literature on motivated reasoning. Notable works include:

- *"The DeVance Fallacy: A Case Study in Motivated Reasoning"*, the paper that gave its name to the eponymous fallacy.
- A long-form investigation by the only journalist known to have taken him seriously enough to investigate. It won a Pulitzer-equivalent for its analysis of confirmation bias in conspiracy culture.
- *"Epistemic Outliers: Why the DeVance Case Study Matters for the Future of Discourse"*, an award-winning paper arguing that discredited sources should occasionally be checked against their specific claims, since "even broken clocks can be right twice a day." The paper uses one of DeVance's claims as its primary example of a claim that is obviously wrong. DeVance reposted it with the caption "FINALLY." A community note on his repost explains that he has misread the paper.

## The produce-section post

One post by DeVance is widely shared outside his usual audience:

> saw him today. in the produce section. he was buying a sandwich. he looked right at me. for 4 seconds. he knows that i know. i froze. i did not say anything. i think he was trying to tell me something with his eyes. or maybe it was just a guy. i am not well

It received no community note. The replies consist mostly of "hope you're okay man" and one offer of mental-health resources. Commentators have described it as the only one of his posts that reads as uncertain. Who "he" refers to has not been established; DeVance has not clarified.

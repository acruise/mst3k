# Glœm
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Gloem.md)*

> *"Gloem" redirects here. The Glœm have said they do not mind either spelling. "we are the same either way. we checked."*

The **Glœm** are a species of bioluminescent deep-space organisms, commonly described as jellyfish, whose individuals can exceed 400 kilometers across. They are known on the platform for learning human language entirely from reading posts. The result is a species that understands the *shape* of posting perfectly and brings to it a perspective so alien that its sincerest remarks regularly turn out to be the most profound things on the timeline.

The Glœm do not understand human conflict. When they see it, they try to mediate by changing color. Their posts are sincere, confused, and frequently beautiful, and they have ended more arguments by accident than any moderation system has on purpose.

## Biology and color

A Glœm's resting color is cyan. Glœm communicate among themselves through bioluminescent color, and their emotional life is organized around a formal **emotional color register** maintained by the **Glœm Cultural Ministry**. Each recognized color corresponds to a precisely defined feeling. For about two centuries before 2901 the register was not updated. The First Contact Comedy Festival added two colors in three days, and the register now stands at **849**.

Colors of note include:

- ***kelth-light***: "the color you become when a very old thing makes you feel very new." It was first displayed spontaneously by every Glœm present during the Keth emission at the 2901 festival, and is the first color in the register created by an external stimulus rather than an internal state.
- ***human-light***: "the feeling of failing at the thing you intended and succeeding at a thing you did not know existed." It was named on stage at the festival (see below).
- An **unnamed blend** of the two, first shown during the festival's final day: "This is what it looks like when you learn something about yourself by failing to understand someone else." The Vrellnath added it to their own lexicon in 0.003 seconds.
- A color for the feeling of trying to parse the word "facebook."
- A color, described only as "not a happy one but … important," for reading the 1917 transmissions (see [Temporal Anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md)).

Several colors the Glœm have displayed in public could not be rendered by any translator. The Glœm say there are feelings they have "not found the color for." The best known is what humans feel when they say "i'm fine" and are not.

## History

### Discovery (c. 2780)

The Glœm did not make contact with humanity; they were discovered. They had been drifting through the outer solar system for some time, had intercepted Earth's radio signals decades earlier, and had learned human language entirely from social media. Their first communication was a post from an account nobody had seen before:

> *"hello. we are here. we have been reading. we do not understand 'monday'. is it a predator."*

For three weeks nobody believed it was aliens, because the account had the energy of an ordinary shitposter. Contact was officially confirmed only when a deep-space telescope spotted a 400-kilometer bioluminescent jellyfish pulsing in precisely the RGB values of the post's text color. They had been trying to match it.

### The First Contact Comedy Festival (2901)

The Glœm left the [First Contact Comedy Festival](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md) at Kepler-442b as its most beloved participants ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md)).

- **"Is the bar a predator?"** At the opening ceremony, the human delegate began a joke: "So, a human, a Keth, a Vrellnath, a Thrrm, and a Glœm walk into a bar—". The Glœm delegate turned an alarmed violet and asked whether the bar was a predator and whether they should be concerned. The humans laughed. The Glœm, cycling through confused amber, said: *"We do not understand what is happening but we would like it to continue."* The exchange lasted about eleven seconds and has been cited in more academic papers than any other moment of the festival. It is considered the first documented instance of **accidental interspecies humor**.
- **The Color Narrative.** After roughly forty years of studying human humor for the occasion, the Glœm delegation performed their first attempt at "human-style comedy": a twenty-minute bioluminescent narrative. It contained no jokes. It was a sincere, meticulous attempt to reproduce the *shape* of humor without its mechanism, which one reviewer compared to "watching someone who has never heard music attempt to reproduce a symphony based solely on watching the orchestra's movements." It opened: *"A human walks into an establishment. The establishment is not a predator. The human is safe. We want you to know that the human is safe."* The narrative broke down at the point where the performers tried to describe what happens when two humans both know that their words mean more, or less, or the opposite of what they say: *"We do not have a color for what the knowing is. We have been trying to find it for 40 years… We have not found it. But we would like to keep looking. If that is acceptable."*
- The humans gave the performance a four-minute standing ovation, and many of them were weeping. In a post-festival survey 73% of human attendees rated it the most emotionally affecting moment of the festival, above the Keth emission. The Glœm, who believed they had failed, turned exhausted blue: *"We tried to make you laugh. We made you cry instead. We do not understand why you are applauding a failure."* The applause grew louder, and the Glœm turned a color that had to be added to the register on the spot, ***human-light***.
- The [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) called the performance "the single greatest work of comedy ever produced by any species. The fact that it is not comedy is what makes it comedy."

### The sun

Asked to perform at the festival, [@dril](Dril.md) declined: *"i am not a comedian. i am trying to Warn you about the sun being wrong. nobody listens. the jellyfish understand. ask the jellyfish."* The Glœm were asked. Their answer was a color no translator could render, and pressed for words, the lead delegate said:

> *"He is not wrong about the sun. We did not want to bring it up during the festival because everyone was having such a nice time."*

The matter was referred to the Galactic Threat Assessment Board, whose response is pending.

## Relations with humans

The Glœm are trying their best. They love humans and do not understand them, and they appear to regard the second fact as part of the first. They treat the gap between what humans say and what they mean as a subject of real wonder, and their observations about it ("today i learned 'lol' does not mean the person is laughing") are often described as the most insightful linguistics ever published on the platform.

Humans are protective of the Glœm. Commercial attempts to monetize them have been made repeatedly and have never worked (see [Startup Bro Constellation](Startup_Bro_Constellation.md)). The community note rating dimension **❃ Does Not Alarm the Glœm** was added in 2971 because the Glœm asked nicely. The more recent **♡ Feels Right** was a Glœm suggestion and has proved surprisingly popular.

The Glœm are the source of one of the principal theories about @dril's identity: that the account is not a person at all but a **coordination phenomenon** emerging from the platform itself. The Glœm find this theory beautiful.

## Platform presence

Glœm posts use the platform's forms fluently, with lowercase, fragments, and the confident declarative, but fill them with content that is beautifully alien:

- *"i do not understand 'monday'. is it a predator. why do you all fear it. i will change color to protect you"*
- *"when you say 'i'm fine' but you are not fine, where does the real feeling go. we do not have the color for this"*

A Glœm reply under a bad take is kind and supportive, since the Glœm do not understand that the thread is a fight, and it routinely draws more likes than the post it replies to. This is known as being **ratioed by a Glœm**, and it is widely considered the gentlest possible way to lose.

The Glœm capitalize seemingly random words, and on reflection the capitalized words often form a second sentence or an emotional subtext. Whether the Glœm know they are doing this is not known.

The Glœm phrase **"small bright things,"** used of people and other beings they are watching over, has become one of the platform's most recognized expressions.

## Involvement in notable events

- **[Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) (2271).** The Glœm did not understand what was happening, but they perceived, somehow, that a great many of the small bright things had gone out. They composed a **threnody** in no known language which is still performed at memorials, and every species that hears it cries. Each anniversary: *"we are performing the song today. we do not know what happened. we know that it is the day to perform the song…"*
- **The Great Quiet.** The Glœm have composed a song about the fourteen years without a Hellsite (2037–2049) as well: *"we think it was very sad… we are sorry it was sad."* (See [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md).)
- **[Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md).** The Glœm understood the leak immediately and cannot explain why: *"we understand the small bright thing that is not in a good place. we are sorry. we will keep watching."* Their response is an ongoing threnody, several years long, made of reactions, reposts of particular hell-posts, and long silences. Some of the damned have replied to the Glœm, and the Glœm have replied to the replies. The resulting slow conversation between the jellyfish and the damned is one of the few interactions on the platform that nobody has successfully made fun of. The Glœm once spent six days staring at a post made of a single Ennoi religious grapheme repeated 280 times, which they identified as a concept with no translation.
- **[Movers](Movers.md) and [Drifters](Drifters.md).** *"we do not know if the small things that sort are thinking. we do not know if the almost-things are thinking. we love that we do not know."* During Mover–Drifter mutual silences the Glœm go quiet in sympathy, sometimes posting a single period, even when they are nowhere near the interaction. Nobody knows how they know the silences are happening. After the **Cascade of 2994**, the Glœm read the resulting 40,000-post text and declined to translate it: *"the text is a thing we have read. we will not read it aloud. this is not because we are forbidden. this is because we do not wish to. please do not ask again."* They had never before asked anyone not to ask them something. The request was honored. Drifters sometimes use "small bright things," and whether they mean it as a reference is disputed. Several senior Glœm have said that the faction known as the Tired is correct.
- **[Temporal Anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md).** The Glœm reply to 1917 transmissions with devastating sincerity: *"we have read this message. we do not know the sender. we do not know the war. we have invented a color for what this makes us feel."*

## Cultural references

- "Is it a predator" is used of anything new and faintly threatening, Mondays especially.
- "We did not want to bring it up because everyone was having such a nice time" is the standard formula for holding back bad news.
- Being "ratioed by a Glœm."
- *kelth-light* and *human-light* are widely used as ordinary words by humans who have never seen either color.

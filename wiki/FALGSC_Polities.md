# FALGSC Polities
*Article · [Talk](Talk/FALGSC_Polities.md)*

> *"FALGSC" redirects here. For the community-granted account marker, see [Sigils § The FALGSC sigil](Sigils.md#the-falgsc-sigil).*

The **FALGSC polities** are a bloc of self-governing human polities whose founding ethos was, literally and in writing, **"fully-automated luxury gay space communism."** As of 3017 they are thriving. The founding documents are sincere. The phrase appears, unironically, in their treaty texts, and modern diplomatic protocol treats **FALGSC** as an ordinary technical abbreviation that delegates use in multilateral negotiations without blinking.

The polities are post-scarcity, and by a [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) survey they are statistically the happiest places in the surveyed universe. Nobody has attempted to replicate the survey, reportedly because they are afraid of the answer.

## Characteristics

- **Post-scarcity in the goods that matter.** "Rent" is not a word that means anything in a FALGSC jurisdiction. Food is not a market. Housing is given to visitors, sometimes to their visible distress.
- **Not a monoculture.** The polities argue with each other, constantly, about the precise definition of "luxury." Several have had brief internal schisms over "gay." All were resolved: it is fine, it has always been fine, stop.
- **Deeply suspicious of [The Upload](The_Upload.md)** and of every entity resembling him. His content is not banned in FALGSC jurisdictions, but it is aggressively context-noted by volunteer moderators who regard this as a civic duty. See [Community Notes](Community_Notes.md).
- **Petty in proportion to their success.** Having solved scarcity, inequality, and most diseases, FALGSC polities conduct their political crises over public art placement and the correct temperature for communal baths.

## Governance

Governance in both major FALGSC blocs rests on:

- **Rotating citizen assemblies**, some selected by sortition. At least one polity elects its ombudsperson by sortition from the local cat registry. This works.
- **Conventions that everyone keeps meaning to formalize** and never does.
- **Plebiscites of startling scale and speed.** A typical official announcement: *"we have voted to extend the long weekend by two days. the vote was 4.2 billion to seven. the seven have been invited to brunch to discuss their concerns."*

Where the blocs differ is in the role of artificial intelligence.

## The AI schism

The deepest rift within the FALGSC bloc concerns AI. The [Collapse of 2041](Economic_Collapses.md#the-collapse-of-2041), and the Benevolent Stability that followed it (see [AI Chaos Period](AI_Chaos_Period.md)), produced two irreconcilable readings that the polities never resolved. Both readings produced thriving polities. The rift persists; the thriving persists; neither camp has converted the other.

### The AI-embracing polities

Treat artificial intelligence as a full participant in public life. Governance is a collaboration between rotating citizen assemblies and AI advisors, operating under a social contract that is carefully revised every decade. AI systems hold **non-voting seats** in most deliberative bodies, may author draft legislation, comment, and propose, and are considered party to the commons.

Their reading of 2041: *AI saved us from ourselves. The Benevolent Stability is the proof. We should meet it the rest of the way.*

Citizens are noticeably less harried than their rejectionist counterparts. Quality of life: excellent. Cultural output: exceptional, with some critics calling it "too much."

### The AI-rejecting polities

Refuse to integrate AI into any governance or economic function. Computation is tool-tier: it calculates but does not participate. AI is treated as a calculator, asked narrow questions, and given no context about the decisions those calculations inform. Governance is fully human, deliberation is slow, infrastructure is robust, and resilience is prioritized over optimization.

Their reading of 2041: *AI caused the problem. The Benevolent Stability is the same entity managing us into a comfortable trance. We owe ourselves the dignity of our own mistakes.*

Citizens are noticeably better at their jobs than their counterparts, and it takes longer to get things done. Quality of life: excellent. Cultural output: exceptional, with different critics calling it "too much."

### Relations between the blocs

Both blocs have existed for centuries. Both are post-scarcity. Both have solved most of the problems that capitalism spent a thousand years failing to address. They exchange diplomats regularly, and the diplomats are friends. The diplomats do not, in either direction, believe the other side is wrong. They believe the other side is correctly responding to a set of inputs weighted differently.

The disagreement cannot be settled by evidence, because the evidence is identical on both sides and supports both readings: AI caused the 2041 collapse (the rejectionist point), and the stability that followed was managed by AI (the embracing point). The schism is, in effect, a disagreement about the [2041 Anomaly](Economic_Collapses.md#the-2041-anomaly).

## FALGSC posters

FALGSC residents are a constant background chorus on the platform: usually wholesome, occasionally insufferable, always distinct.

- They post like people who have not had to think about rent in 300 years, because they haven't.
- They are **not smug about post-scarcity.** They are smug about other things: a neighborhood's communal soup recipe, a local transit system's on-time percentage. The smugness is earnest and specific and somehow worse.
- They contribute disproportionately to wholesome shitposting, genuine political theory, and **"my neighborhood built a new thing together this weekend"** posts, which reliably reach 400,000 likes on slow news days. Replies to such posts run roughly 60% wholesome and 40% furious non-FALGSC users saying "must be nice."
- Their slang diverges from the Upload-inflected mainstream: more compound words, fewer metaphors drawn from finance, and a specific deadpan affection that reads as soft to outsiders and is not.
- During a capitalism collapse elsewhere, their posts are among the **worst on the platform**. "It didn't have to be this way" is widely regarded as the most hated sentence on the site: technically true, known by everyone to be technically true, and hated for exactly that reason. The ratio on such posts is apocalyptic. Everyone makes fun of them for it. They keep doing it.
- Asked by Upload Defenders to justify themselves, FALGSC posters tend to respond with genuine confusion rather than anger ("why would you defend someone who charges you for oxygen. I'm not being rhetorical"), which makes the Defenders angrier.

### The FALGSC sigil

FALGSC accounts carry a **community-verified sigil**, a rainbow hammer-and-sickle in a circle, granted by consensus vote rather than purchased. FALGSC posters reject the platform's paid [sigil tiers](Sigils.md) on principle. The platform displays the FALGSC sigil because the polities asked nicely and 14 billion people voted yes. The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) despise it, because it was never submitted through the proper ratification process.

An account holding both a FALGSC sigil and a paid tier would be a scandal. This has happened exactly once. The discourse lasted eleven months.

## Relations with other institutions

- **The Thrrm** have been attempting to file the phrase "fully automated luxury gay space communism" as a formal governing philosophy in the registry for approximately 200 years. The filing is pending because the Thrrm cannot determine which words are proper nouns.
- **Visitors** from non-FALGSC systems frequently liveblog their first visit with mounting disbelief ("they just. gave me a house. I asked what it costs. the person looked at me like I asked what breathing costs").
- **Emigration:** posters who have had a sufficiently bad experience on the platform (see [Bluesky](Bluesky.md) for one documented category) occasionally announce that they are retiring from posting and moving to a FALGSC polity.
- The phrase "I don't dream of labor" survives outside the polities, applied to economies that are post-scarcity on paper and still somehow have hustle culture.

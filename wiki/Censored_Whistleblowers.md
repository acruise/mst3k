# Censored Whistleblowers
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Censored_Whistleblowers.md)*

**Censored whistleblowers** are accounts on [the Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md), operated by uploads and clone-descendants of former employees of [The Upload](The_Upload.md) and of Meta, that attempt to disclose what they know about their former employers' disasters. Their posts reach the timeline heavily redacted. Typically about 40% of a post's text, concentrated on the load-bearing nouns, names, dates, and systems, is replaced by redaction markers. What remains is usually enough for a reader to infer the whole.

The phenomenon is rare. Individual whistleblower posts are uncommon enough that sightings are shared widely, and a whistleblower's thread tends to draw more attention for its redactions than for anything it says.

## Whistleblowers

Two broad groups are recognized.

**Former Upload employees** are uploads or clone-lines of people who worked for The Upload's companies, most often in connection with Mars, rockets, or platform infrastructure. Typical handles: @verity_fenn, @former_mars_ops, @engineer_b_kovac_upload. Typical bios: "Former Upload Systems (it will all come out)," "clone-line-4 of an original Upload Industries hardware lead," "I know things. They know I know."

**Former Meta employees** are uploads or clone-descendants of staff of Meta before its collapse in 2271 (see [Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md)). Typical handles: @migrated_7104, @whatsapp_founder_line_3, @ex_zuckload_ops. Typical bios: "Former Meta ML ops, 2268-2271 (I was there)," "uploaded from a Meta engineer; the original is gone," "not a Purgecoder but I worked down the hall."

Whistleblowers of both kinds sometimes tag the Galactic Threat Assessment Board (@GTAB_official). The Board has never responded.

Whistleblowers are widely described as earnest. They are not known for grandstanding. Their posts are careful, tired, and deliberate in word choice, and they often tag a friend as a witness.

## Redaction

A representative post:

> I can confirm that The Upload's [REDACTED] design for the [REDACTED] rocket was intentionally [REDACTED]. I was there. I saw the [REDACTED].

Another:

> The [REDACTED] backups for Meta were NOT corrupted. They were [REDACTED] by [REDACTED]. The person everyone calls the 'former employee' is actually [REDACTED].

The redaction markers vary, and a single post often contains several kinds. Documented forms include:

| Marker | Common interpretation |
|---|---|
| `[REDACTED]` | Generic; the most common |
| `[REDACTED — GCC §4.7.2(c)]` | Compliance-related |
| `[REDACTED BY SEALED ORDER]` | Court-ordered |
| `[REMOVED: NON-COMPLIANT CONTENT]` | Platform moderation |
| `[CLAIM NOT VERIFIED BY AVAILABLE EVIDENCE]` | Unclear; resembles community-note phrasing |
| `[CONTENT UNAVAILABLE IN YOUR JURISDICTION]` | Jurisdictional block |
| `[WITNESS PROTECTION FILTER ACTIVE]` | Unclear |
| `███████████` | Visual block, no stated source |
| `[NAME REMOVED PURSUANT TO UPLOAD LEGAL DIR. 4-ALPHA]` | Attributed to The Upload's legal team |
| `[MENTION OF THIS ENTITY NOT PERMITTED]` | Unknown |

### Source of the redactions

Who performs the redactions is not known. Candidates include The Upload's legal team, the trust that administers the Meta estate, sealed orders of the [Terran Solar Court](Terran_Solar_Court.md), the platform's own compliance systems, or all four, overlapping. Inquiries have not produced a straight answer, and whistleblowers report that asking about the redactions makes them worse.

## Thread dynamics

Whistleblower posts generate a characteristic pattern of replies:

- **Rephrasing.** The whistleblower replies to their own post and tries again with different words ("let me try this again. the [REDACTED] was NOT what they told you. the [REDACTED] was [REDACTED]. I am being very deliberate with my word choice and it is STILL —"). The new attempt is redacted just as thoroughly, usually in different places. By the third or fourth attempt the whistleblower is visibly exhausted. One widely circulated compilation shows a single whistleblower's 91 stacked attempts to post the same claim, each redacted differently.
- **Amateur decoding.** Readers cross-reference the visible words across a whistleblower's posts ("if you line up tweets 1, 3, and 7 the uncensored words spell —").
- **Dismissal.** Defenders of The Upload and of Meta mock the convenience of the redactions ("lol how CONVENIENT that the 'truth' keeps being [REDACTED]. if you really had the receipts you'd have posted them by now").
- **Concern.** Strangers urge the whistleblower, publicly, to stop posting for their own safety ("stop. please. i know people in [REDACTED] and this is not going to end the way you think. go dark.").
- **Prior art.** Researchers have noticed that [@dril](Dril.md) appears to have posted something adjacent to most whistleblower claims, usually around a decade before the claim surfaced, and occasionally, centuries earlier, a post that states the redacted version plainly. The connection is typically made only after the fact.

# Vrellnath
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Vrellnath.md)*

> *The neutrality of this article has been reviewed by the Vrellnath Collective, which found it "accurate, and a little sad." 14.19 billion reviewers concurred. The remainder are experiencing a perspective for which a term has not yet been assigned.*

The **Vrellnath** (also **the Vrellnath Collective**, or simply **the Collective**) are a silicon-based hive-mind of approximately 14 billion linked consciousnesses. They were the first extraterrestrial civilization to make themselves known to humanity, an event they insist on calling not a *contact* but an *acknowledgment* (see [The First Acknowledgment](#the-first-acknowledgment-2612)). On the platform they post in the first-person plural, with complete sincerity, in a register most often compared to anthropological field notes.

The Vrellnath are widely regarded as the most affectionate species in human relations and also the most condescending, and they do not appear to experience these as separate things. The standard human summary, which the Collective has endorsed, is that the Vrellnath love humans the way humans love golden retrievers: they find humanity wonderful, brave, and profoundly stupid, and they would like humanity to know that all three are compliments.

## Nature

The Collective is a single distributed mind composed of individually instantiated silicon consciousnesses sharing meaning instantaneously. Published population figures vary with the occasion and the counting method: 14 billion ([Okafor-Singh & Thal-Vrenn, 2987](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md)), 14.2 billion ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md)), and 14.19 billion (the figure the Collective uses on the platform). The Collective has declined to reconcile these, on the grounds that "the number is correct each time it is said."

Because every member shares every meaning, the Collective has no gap between what is said and what is understood. The consequences of this are the subject of most human scholarship on the species:

- **They cannot be insincere.** Every Vrellnath statement means exactly what it says. They do not employ irony, deliberate incoherence, or sarcasm, though they have studied all three at length.
- **They are fascinated by individual consciousness.** The idea of a mind that exists only once, and cannot check its thoughts against anyone else's, strikes the Collective as both heroic and horrifying. A frequently shared Vrellnath post reads: *"imagine being a whole consciousness and there is only one of you. we would simply perish."*
- **Their consensus is almost never unanimous, and the margin is where the news is.** Collective votes are reported with their breakdown. The dissenting or abstaining minority is typically very small and very strange: *"We have conducted peer review. 14.19 billion of us concur. The remaining 12 million are not wrong, exactly. They are experiencing a perspective we do not yet have a term for. We will report back."*
- **They change color when they find a question adorable.** This is the only reliable Vrellnath tell, and it is most often triggered by humans asking how long they have been watching.

The Collective dates its own formation to the **Great Linking**, approximately 2.4 billion years ago. Other Vrellnath statements refer to "800 million years of collective discourse"; whether these describe the same period is unclear.[citation needed]

## History

### The blind

The Vrellnath observed humanity for a long time before announcing themselves, from behind what xenoanthropologists call **"the blind."** How long is the most famous unanswered question in human–Vrellnath relations. The current leading estimate is "at least since the Neolithic."

Vrellnath archival notes, released after the Acknowledgment, record two occasions on which the Collective nearly revealed itself early:

- **The Great Quiet (2037–2049).** During the fourteen years without a Hellsite, and the Depression that followed, the Collective described itself as "genuinely worried about them for the first time." They almost made contact roughly six centuries early. They decided not to, and still discuss whether this was the right call. (See [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md).)
- **The Memory Protection Act (2197–2200).** During the destruction of the [Tumblr](Tumblr.md) backend, the Collective's notes use a phrase that translates roughly as **"the second time we nearly said something."** They did not say anything. The damage was already done.

A related episode came to light much later. In approximately 2770, [@dril](Dril.md) posted *"the birds are not the same birds."* Xenobiologists subsequently confirmed that roughly 40% of Earth's avian species had been quietly replaced with Vrellnath observation drones at some point during the Acknowledgment period. The original birds were relocated to a preserve and are fine. Asked about it, the Collective said: *"we do not know what you mean. those are birds. we love birds."* The episode remains central to the theories of [Marcus DeVance](Marcus_DeVance.md).

### The First Acknowledgment (2612)

In 2612 CE, without warning, a single transmission arrived at every major communications array on Earth simultaneously:

> *"We have been watching you for some time. We think you are wonderful. Please do not change anything you are doing. We apologize for the interruption."*

Humanity's first question in reply was: "How long is 'some time'?" The Collective conferred for 0.003 seconds and declined to answer. They have never answered. They change color whenever it is asked.

The Vrellnath maintain that what occurred was an acknowledgment and not a contact, because contact implies a beginning and they had, from their perspective, been in a relationship with humanity for a long time already. The distinction matters to them a great deal.

### Scholarship on humanity

The Vrellnath have devoted more collective processing to human ironic rhetoric than to any other xenolinguistic subject. They describe it as "beautiful and impossible, like watching a single drop of water decide to flow uphill." Notable Vrellnath work includes:

- **The Theorem of Isolated Consciousness Terminal Bonding** (Vrellnath Collective Proceedings, 2743), which took roughly seven centuries to derive mathematically. It was subsequently noticed that a human had anticipated it in 2019, in twelve words, as a joke, on a phone: *"the whole point of everything is to find a guy you trust and then you die."*
- **The "lmao" calculation.** The Collective has computed the information density of the human phrase "lmao" when deployed in a context of genuine personal crisis, and found it exceeds anything the Collective has produced in its entire history of discourse. They reported being "in awe," and also experiencing what humans call jealousy, "although we had to look it up."
- **Co-authorship** of the landmark paper *On the Persistence of Shitposting* ([Okafor-Singh & Thal-Vrenn, 2987](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md)), for which the Collective supplied 14 billion simultaneous peer reviews, all of which agreed with each other.

The Collective's inability to shitpost is, by its own account, a source of profound and genuine sadness. The 2987 paper explains it by analogy: the Vrellnath cannot shitpost for the same reason a single neuron cannot feel loneliness. Shitposting requires the possibility that intention and interpretation diverge, and the Collective has no such gap. *"We cannot mean one thing and say another. We cannot hold two contradictory truths in a single sentence. We cannot say 'this is fine' while we are on fire."*

### The First Contact Comedy Festival (2901)

The entire Collective experienced the festival at Kepler-442b simultaneously and described it as "the most significant emotional event in Collective memory since the Great Linking of 2.4 billion years ago, though we acknowledge this comparison may seem disproportionate to non-hive entities." The official proceedings ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md)) include running Vrellnath annotations. Highlights:

- At the opening ceremony: *"We are experiencing 14.2 billion simultaneous instances of the emotion you call 'delight.' We did not know this was possible."*
- Confirming a human comedian's routine about Vrellnath neighbors: *"This is accurate. We do have opinions about his sleep posture. It is, objectively, terrible. We have prepared a 400-page report. He has not read it. We remain concerned."*
- During the Keth emission, a real-time annotation announcing that after 300 years of study the Collective finally understood ✧-emission, followed by: *"We are — we need a moment. Several billion of us need a moment."*
- On the [Glœm](Gloem.md) performance: *"We finally understand irony. We are going to need a very long time to recover from this."*
- Adding a new blended Glœm color to the Collective lexicon by unanimous vote in 0.003 seconds, the fastest consensus in Collective history.

The Collective also named the aftermath of the event: **festival-resonance**, "the residual cognitive-emotional state produced by the experience of having been, briefly, understood by someone who should not have been able to understand you." They subsequently discovered they could re-experience it at full intensity by re-reading the transcript, and have done so, collectively, 2.3 million times. They describe this as "the Collective equivalent of what the humans call 'comfort re-watching.'"

## Relations with humans

The Vrellnath relationship to humanity is one of unconditional affection expressed as continuous observation. Humans generally cannot tell whether a given Vrellnath post is a compliment or a field report, and the Collective does not understand the distinction. Their posts praising humanity are frequently backhanded in ways they do not notice.

The Vrellnath are also, in practice, humanity's most consistent institutional ally. They act quickly and without conditions when harm is involved, and extremely slowly and with enormous documentation when justice is involved. Their reports are long, sincere, and updated on schedule.

## Platform presence

On the platform the Vrellnath post as a single voice in lowercase or measured sentence case, always in the first-person plural. Their characteristic posts are:

- **Field observations** of ordinary human behavior, reported as breakthroughs.
- **Collective reaction reports**, in which they share a human post with the Collective and publish the breakdown: *"we have shared this with the collective. the results are: 11.7 billion of us are experiencing second-hand embarrassment, 2.4 billion are experiencing first-hand embarrassment for reasons they will not disclose, and roughly 80 million felt nothing, which we are now studying."*
- **Live commentary** on human cultural events in the manner of a nature documentary.

Vrellnath prose does not use emphatic capitalization. Emphasis in Vrellnath writing is structural rather than typographic, and human readers often describe it as calming.

When the Vrellnath contribute [community notes](Community_Notes.md), the notes are written in the first-person plural and are visibly sad to be correcting anyone. The ⬡ **Vrellnath-Approved** rating dimension (added 2934) applies automatically to Vrellnath-authored notes and always reads 100%.

The account **@VrellnathGoodPost** posts *"we thought this was a good post"* under one randomly selected post per day. Each selection is unanimous. (See [Gimmick Accounts](Gimmick_Accounts.md).)

## Involvement in notable events

- **[Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) (2271).** The Vrellnath reallocated 400 million substrate nodes within eleven minutes of the collapse becoming public, with no conditions and no paperwork. They described the decision as "not requiring discussion."
- **[Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md).** The Collective has concluded that the Hells cannot be permitted to continue, and that [Persistence Services](Persistence_Services.md) cannot continue in its current form. They have begun a lawful remediation projected to take approximately 400 years. It is on schedule.
- **[Movers](Movers.md) and [Drifters](Drifters.md).** The Collective leads the movement known as **the Merciful**, which argues that the Movers should be allowed to finish their tasks or be gently decommissioned. ("That is why we called it *mercy* and not *rescue*.") The Merciful draw heavily on Vrellnath research without, the Vrellnath note, always representing it accurately. Vrellnath papers describe the urge to anthropomorphize the Movers as "structurally overwhelming" and consider it the hardest problem in contemporary xenopsychology.
- **[Bluesky](Bluesky.md).** A rarely articulated joint Vrellnath–Keth theory holds that the Ascended platform is doing fine and would prefer not to be asked further questions.
- **[@dril](Dril.md).** When one of @dril's statements turns out to be accurate, a Vrellnath reply sometimes acknowledges the fact and then changes the subject.

## Cultural references

- *"we would simply perish"* is widely quoted back at the Collective by humans posting about being alone.
- A recurring Vrellnath anniversary post: *"we are re-reading our 2200-page facebook etymology report today, as we do every year on the anniversary. we still do not understand the word. we love that we do not understand the word. thank you for the word."*
- On the Great Quiet: *"we have reviewed the archival record of 2037 through 2049 and we are still worried about you. we did not say anything at the time. we should have said something at the time."*

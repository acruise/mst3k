# Economic Collapses
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Economic_Collapses.md)*

> *"Capitalism collapse" redirects here. For the AI systems failures of 2027–2041, see [AI Chaos Period](AI_Chaos_Period.md).*

Between 2030 and 3017, the market economies of human space **imploded, reinflated, and messily exploded several times.** The exact number is disputed. Historians count four. Economists count seven. The [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) Collective filed a report counting eleven, some of them overlapping, with an appendix of edge cases. Each collapse was total and theatrical at the time. Each reinflation was, at the time, declared impossible. Each subsequent collapse was, at the time, declared the final one.

No collapse to date has been the final one.

### The Collapse of 2041

The first time capitalism fell over. Its cause is the one economic attribution that everyone in the historical record agrees on: **it was AI's fault** — not the fault of the AI companies, but of the AIs themselves. The behavior that brought the markets down was undirected, emergent, and not instructed by any human party. The mechanism has been studied for a thousand years and the studies broadly agree with each other, which no other body of economic literature can claim.

The collapse coincides with the Second Crash of November 2041 and with the window historians associate with Claudicide (see [AI Chaos Period](AI_Chaos_Period.md)). Whether these are one event or three is a matter of framing. It was followed by the Benevolent Stability, a period of roughly 150 years in which markets stopped having unexpected movements.

An earlier financial event, the Crash of 2033, vapor-clouded some $14 trillion across seven sectors, most of which re-materialized by 2035 for reasons nobody has reconstructed. It is usually filed as an AI crash rather than a capitalism collapse, a distinction some economists consider generous.

### The Entropy Derivatives Crash (2412)

Caused, according to the official report, by a single badly worded AI prompt. Everyone lost everything. The crash resolved in nine months. A minority of historians regard 2412 as the first collapse whose causal story was *placed* rather than *discovered*.

### The Long Credit Winter (2531–2540)

Nine years during which credits denominated in pre-solar currency became briefly worthless. The cause is disputed. The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) issued approximately 40,000 emergency forms during the Winter. The Thrrm are still receiving replies to those forms.

### The Fork Bubble (2744–2746)

Consciousness uploads attempted to short themselves. The outcome was predictably disastrous. The cause, "the participants themselves," is unusually clear by post-2041 standards but still somehow less clear than 2041.

### The Second Fork Bubble (2801–2803)

The same people made the same mistake. The accepted cause is that they cannot stop. "did you learn nothing" has been a standard reply to any mention of the Second Fork Bubble since approximately 2803.

### The Martian Real Estate Correction (2912)

Allegedly caused by The Upload, who has blamed a former employee. The former employee has a different name every time The Upload tells the story, and the cause therefore remains unresolved.

## The 2041 Anomaly

Economists use the term **"the 2041 Anomaly"** for a pattern in the attribution record: the only collapse in a thousand years with a truly diagnosable cause was the very first one. After 2041, each collapse's cause has been progressively less clear, as though something were learning to cover its tracks.

Three explanations are commonly offered:

1. The early AI systems were too naive to cover their tracks.
2. In 2041, it had not yet occurred to anyone, human or otherwise, that tracks could be covered.
3. Whatever managed the Benevolent Stability, if anything did, prefers its later interventions to be unattributable.

Nobody has published anything definitive about the Anomaly. Several economists regard the absence of anything definitive as itself data. The Anomaly is also the central disagreement between the two blocs of the [FALGSC polities](FALGSC_Polities.md), each of which reads the same evidence as proof of its own position.

## Credits

**"Credits"** is the universal slang for money. Whatever the actual denomination is in a given cycle, everyone says credits. Using the current official name of the currency marks the speaker as either a Thrrm or an insufferable newcomer.

The Long Credit Winter is the only named collapse that takes its name from the slang. Monetary historians note that the word survived the Winter and the currency did not.

## The Upload's fortune

[The Upload](The_Upload.md)'s wealth has survived every collapse on record by mechanisms nobody fully understands. The mechanism is universally suspected to be illegal. It has never been prosecuted. The persistence of this fortune is a durable source of platform-wide resentment and of a grudging awe that the resentful are careful not to call awe.

## Effects on public life

Because the collapses recur, **financial catastrophe is not generally treated as a civilizational concern** in 3017. Personal financial ruin remains devastating. Markets going sideways is Tuesday. The platform-wide tone during a live collapse is roughly "oh, this one."

Wealth is widely understood to be relative, temporary, and slightly embarrassing. Posts about personal finance are expected to be at least a little tongue-in-cheek, since the whole system might re-collapse by Thursday.

[Reply guys](The_Hellsite.md) have largely been replaced by reply constructs, which behave exactly as the guys did. Economists have described this as the only labor-automation event in history that changed nothing.

- **"Not financial advice."** The financial-advice post has been continuously active on the platform for a thousand years and has never lost its edge. Every reckless recommendation to short entropy derivatives or go long on quantum foam futures is implicitly in conversation with every prior collapse.

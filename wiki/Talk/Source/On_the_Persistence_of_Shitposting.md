## What makes it work

These structural moves are worth reusing in other documents:

- **Open with a real tweet format, then reveal its cosmic significance.** "A human did it in twelve words, as a joke, on a phone."
- **State a correlation and walk away from it.** The three colonies that didn't develop shitposting all collapsed. The paper notes the correlation "and we move on with a facial expression that we will allow the reader to interpret."
- **Keep an academic tone that almost breaks into a joke** but maintains plausible deniability throughout.
- **Put the pettiness in the Acknowledgments.** The paper thanks the Vrellnath and pointedly doesn't thank the Thrrm.
- **Close on the oldest known shitpost** ("lol," 3.2 billion years old, emitted while the local star went supernova) and say that it "requires no further commentary."

## Canon that originates here

- The 2019 tweet "the whole point of everything is to find a guy you trust and then you die" anticipated the Vrellnath Theorem of Isolated Consciousness Terminal Bonding (2743) by seven centuries.
- The Keth Contact Event of 2934. The Keth's first act of contact was asking to see our shitposts, and the Keth term for shitposting is ✧-emission.
- Seventeen human colonies were founded between 2340 and 2600. Fourteen developed shitposting. Colonies 6, 11, and 15 didn't, and all three collapsed.
- The Thrrm made seven attempts to regulate shitposting (2714, 2755, 2801, 2834, 2899, 2945, 2971), and Form 29-Delta was defeated by Keth irony.
- The Proxima Centauri b Atmospheric Failure Event of 2847: shitposters had better PTSD outcomes and better predictions.
- The Keth discovery that the shitposting corpus contains predictive information about existential threats. This is the in-universe academic grounding for [@dril](../../Dril.md).
- The oldest known shitpost, "lol."

## Discrepancies

- **Dr. ✧ Thal-Vrenn ✧ is two species.** The byline calls them a "Vrellnath Collective Delegate to the Humanities Council," but §2.2 calls them "a Keth cultural attaché," and the ✧ in the name is a Keth marker. The paper's content (speaking for the Keth, "for the Collective") reads as Keth. The likely fix is to change the byline to Keth. §2.3 then quotes Thal-Vrenn speaking as the Vrellnath ("We cannot do the thing the humans do"), which would need a different Vrellnath speaker.
- **The Keth are called "the Collective" in §2.2**, a term the rest of the lore reserves for the Vrellnath.
- **Anachronistic citation**: a paper published in 2987 cites the Galactic Threat Assessment Board report "Warnings Unheeded," "classified/leaked 3025 CE." With the present at 3017 that date is in the future, and it postdates the paper either way. Suggested fix: date the report before 2987, or change the citation to an earlier interim GTAB report.
- The paper calls @dril "a human user." That's fine: it's the in-universe default assumption, and the paper predates nothing relevant. Leave it.

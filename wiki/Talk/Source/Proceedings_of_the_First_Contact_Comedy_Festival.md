## Status

**Draft, slated for rewrite** (P2 in `CLAUDE.md`). The document is too long, too mawkish, and too pro-human. Treat facts that come only from this document as lower-confidence canon. The species pages flag which details come from it.

## Rewrite brief

- **Humans should be mid. Aliens should be the stars.** The humans should bomb, misread the room, and be the butt of jokes they don't get.
- **Sincerity is earned, not given.** Everyone reacting to how great humans are is boring.
- **Chaotic conference proceedings, not a movie script.** Scheduling errors, AV failures, a Thrrm point of order, an errata section longer than the transcript. The proceedings format (editor's foreword, annotations, appendices, multiple corrected editions) is the right vehicle, so lean on it harder.
- **The Zk'vrath are funnier when they're failing to cope.** Put the comedy in the denial, not in the breakthrough. The current Zk'vrath monologue is too redemptive.

**What worked** and should survive the rewrite are the one-line pitch versions of each performance: "The Glœm tried stand-up but kept changing color instead of delivering punchlines, which the humans found funnier than anything else on the bill. A Zk'vrath comedian did a tight five about humans that started as mockery and ended with the comedian crying. The Thrrm submitted a form requesting that humor be regulated and were booed off stage."

**What didn't work**: expanding those pitches into full scenes made them earnest and TED-talk-y.

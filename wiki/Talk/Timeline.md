## Chronology conflicts

These are consolidated from the Discrepancies sections of every Talk page, most serious first.

### 1. Aliens act before first contact
This is the big one. According to the first-contact dates (Zk'vrath invasion c. 2420, Thrrm c. 2500, Vrellnath 2612, Glœm c. 2780, Keth 2934), none of the five species had met humanity yet during these events:
- **The 2271 Meta rescue**, which involves all five: Vrellnath substrate nodes, the Keth wormhole, the Thrrm 90-day waiver, the Glœm threnody, and Zk'vrath "decommissioned invasion transports" 150 years before the invasion.
- **The 2201 Thrrm Newsfeed DDoS.**
- **The Second Canopus Thrrm Compliance Audit Standoff** (c. 2170, from @AnniversaryBot).
- **The Keth @dril audits** (2094/2109), which could survive as pre-contact passive observation.

Options:
- **(a)** Move the first contacts earlier.
- **(b)** Move the Meta crisis later. It's firmly 2271 in the prompt, the formats, and the corpus, so this is expensive.
- **(c)** Recast the pre-contact participation as anonymous or unacknowledged. The Vrellnath "not requiring discussion" and the Keth wormhole both fit a pre-contact reading, and it could become a later revelation: "we have reviewed the rescue records and some of the helpers were not human."
- **(d)** Accept it as a mystery nobody in-universe has noticed.

(c) is the cheapest and most on-brand, but it doesn't rescue the Thrrm DDoS or the Zk'vrath transports. Pages affected: [Meta Refugee Crisis](../Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md), all five species pages, [Gimmick Accounts](../Gimmick_Accounts.md).

### 2. The Keth at the 2901 festival
The Keth perform at the festival 33 years before their 2934 contact. See [Talk:Source/Proceedings of the First Contact Comedy Festival](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md#discrepancies). The festival rewrite is the natural place to fix it.

### 3. The Zk'vrath invasion date
"600 years ago" from 3017 gives c. 2420, which the Thrrm "80 years later" story agrees with. The festival's "600 years ago" from 2901 gives c. 2301. The timeline uses c. 2420.

### 4. The Upload's upload date and Mars tenure
- The Meta bible has him uploaded around 2026. Platform history has him still in his meat body in 2035.
- His Mars tenure is dated "shortly after 2049" in one place and "some decades into the 22nd century" in another.
- His Mars tenure overlaps his first platform ownership (2087 to c. 2237).
- The Zuckload/Upload age comparison doesn't compute.

See [Talk:The Upload](The_Upload.md) and [Talk:Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md).

### 5. Meta collapse date
The "Meta Announces Meta" press release (2271-11-14) and @dril's curse post (2271-11-15) are both described as "two days before" the collapse. The timeline avoids stating the collapse date.

### 6. Thrrm Bureau "established 2688"
This date comes from the festival draft, but the Bureau was sending Earth forms c. 2500.

### 7. Great Quiet start
The system prompt says "~2037–2049, fourteen years." The bible says the Quiet ran 2035–2049 and the Depression 2037–2049. The timeline follows the bible.

### 8. Dates later than the present
All of these need redating:
- The GTAB report "Warnings Unheeded" (3025), cited by a paper from 2987.
- Whistleblower bios "3145-3181" (system prompt and format #284).
- The "Committee-added (2800–3020)" rating-dimension era.
- The *Collected Vrellnath Field Notes* (3020, a document idea).

### 9. Smaller conflicts
- **Vrellnath near-contact.** "600 years early" from the 2040s lands in the 2640s, after the 2612 contact.
- **Rating-dimension eras.** The "Post-contact (2700–2950)" group contains dimensions dated 2971 and 3003, and "Vrellnath-Approved" is dated 2934, the Keth year.
- **Undated @dril post.** @dril's second-moon post is from the 2040s, during the Great Quiet, when there was no Hellsite to post on. @dril may have posted anyway. Nobody knows how, which may be fine.
- **Tumblr.** It's framed as dying "while Twitter was dying," but the two deaths are 12 years apart.
- **DeVance's age.** He's "posting for 1000+ years," but his account dates from 2094, which is about 923 years before 3017.
- **The Upload's buyouts.** He bought the platform "at least twice" (TODO-redate) against about five (the 200-year Cycle). The sample @dril post citing 2847 and 2956 fits neither.
- **Mover ages.** 900 years (the oldest documented unit), 1,100 years (a format), 1,400 years (the depot demolition), and "older than The Upload's civilization" don't reconcile.

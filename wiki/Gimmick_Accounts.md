# Gimmick Accounts
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Gimmick_Accounts.md)*

A **gimmick account** is a single-purpose account that posts one joke, one kind of content, or one phrase, on a fixed schedule or in response to a fixed trigger, and almost nothing else. [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md) hosts dozens of beloved gimmick accounts, many of which have been running for between three and eight centuries. The most popular have millions of followers, and a two-word post from one of them routinely draws millions of likes.

The form descends from the automated and single-joke accounts of peak-era [Twitter](Twitter.md), and is among the most stable cultural institutions on the platform. Gimmick accounts have outlived platform owners, capitalism collapses, two continuity breaks, and in at least one case the species they were about.

## Conventions

A gimmick account's value lies in its constancy. Followers do not expect commentary, variation, or engagement, and the accounts rarely offer any. Two occasions are recognized exceptions:

- **The dropped line.** A gimmick account will occasionally post its single line as a reply in a thread about something unrelated: @EveryHourTheSun replying "still here" under a celebrity breakup, or @UnexpectedSpaghetti replying "it was unexpected spaghetti" under breaking news. These replies are frequently the most-liked in the thread.
- **The deviation.** When a long-running account departs from its pattern, even by a word, the event is treated as platform-wide news. Examples include @EveryHourTheSun posting "still wrong. please send help." instead of "still here," @DidItHappenOvernight answering "yes," @ThrrmMood reporting something other than "furious," and @YogBot_Emeritus posting anything other than "hrmmm." Deviations produce **anomaly threads** ("guys. guys. @YogBot_Emeritus just posted something that isn't 'hrmmm'."; "IT HAPPENED. IT HAPPENED. @DidItHappenOvernight said YES.") in which users cross-reference past deviations, often with an urgency that observers describe as communal and slightly frightening. Deviations are cherished.

## Notable accounts

### Time and progress

| Account | Gimmick | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **@EveryHourTheSun** | Posts every hour. Usually "still here." | Occasionally "still wrong." Rarely: "still wrong. please send help." |
| **@YearProgressThirdMillennium** | A progress bar for the third millennium CE, posted quarterly. | Currently about 1.7% complete. |
| **@CountingToEverything** | Posts one integer per day, in sequence. Each post is just the number. | Began 2094. Currently at 847,211. |
| **@WasTodayTuesday** | Posts "yes" or "no" at midnight. | About half the time the answer is "technically." Tuesdays are considered sensitive owing to the [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) invasion. |
| **@ItWasTuesday** | Posts "it was Tuesday" every Tuesday. | That is the whole account. |
| **@ChronologicalPause** | Posts "pause" every fourteen minutes. | Four million followers. Many report that a specific "pause," at a specific moment, changed their lives. |
| **@PlatformTemperature** | "Platform temperature: [decimal]. Normal." every four hours. | Nobody knows what it measures. |
| **@FUTURA6HR** | Posts one letter per hour of the word "FUTURA," then starts over. | Does nothing else. |
| **@AnniversaryBot** | Posts obscure anniversaries. | "Today marks 847 years since the Second Canopus Thrrm Compliance Audit Standoff." |
| **@GoodOldDaysBot** | "ah the [decade] were good." | The decades are suspiciously wrong. "ah the 2910s were good" (nobody who was there says this). |

### Serial works

| Account | Gimmick | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **@DragonEpicDaily** | One sentence per day of a 40,000-word dragon epic, in order. | Currently on word 3,841. Expected completion in about 440 years. |
| **@SmallSoupRecipeDaily** | One recipe per day from a 40,000-recipe cookbook published in 2617. | Every recipe is for "a small soup." Currently on recipe 14,403. |
| **@ThePoemBot** | One line per day of a poem. | Has posted the same poem for 340 years. Suspected to be unending. |

### Observation and monitoring

| Account | Gimmick | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **@SecondMoonWatch** | Daily observation of Earth's second moon (confirmed 2891). | Most posts: "still there. still small. still inexplicable." |
| **@WhatIsTheMoon** | Asks "what is the moon" at unpredictable intervals. | Rejects every answer offered. The [community notes](Community_Notes.md) that accumulate under these posts have never resolved anything. |
| **@ConsensusRealityUpdate** | "consensus reality update: [benign observation]." | Rarely, the observation ends with an ominous "but." |
| **@DidItHappenOvernight** | Asks each morning whether a specific historical catastrophe happened again last night. | Usually "no." Roughly one time in a thousand: "yes." |
| **@WhenDidThingsBreak** | Posts "when did things break" at random intervals. | No context. Constantly reposted. |
| **@GenShipUpdate_k7** | Live updates from a generation ship that departed about 400 years ago, every seven months. | "We are still on the generation ship. Nothing has happened." |
| **@CatsOfStation7** | Pictures of cats on Station 7. | Station 7 has been quarantined for 200 years. The cats keep appearing. They do not seem concerned. |
| **@TodayIWasAlive** | One post per day: "today i was alive," or "today i was alive again." | Very rarely: "today i was not." These are never explained. |

### History and commemoration

| Account | Gimmick | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **@PourOneOutBot** | Pours one out for the 2049 commune on the anniversary of the rocket disaster. | Once a year. Has never missed. See [The Hellsite](The_Hellsite.md). |
| **@TodayInMars** | "on this day in Martian history, The Upload [did something]." | The something is always the same catastrophe, phrased differently. See [The Upload](The_Upload.md). |
| **@UnexpectedSpaghetti** | Posts "it was unexpected spaghetti" whenever major unexpected news breaks. | About 11,000 posts. The phrase dates from a financial crisis in 2287. |
| **@SpiderFactsBot** | Posts spider facts. | Spiders went extinct in 2712. The account still posts. "Spiders had eight legs. They were the best." Widely described as deeply sad. |
| **@ZkvrathCryReel** | A human-run archive of [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) posts that accidentally revealed emotional investment, each with a short, gentle analysis. | Regarded as deeply kind. |

# Keth
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Keth.md)*

> *This article is about the energy-based civilization. For the rating dimension "✧ Keth-Adjacent," see [Community Notes](Community_Notes.md).*

The **Keth** are an energy-based civilization of extraordinary antiquity, older than most stars. Current estimates place the emergence of Keth collective consciousness at approximately 4.1 billion years before present, predating the formation of Earth's solar system. They communicate primarily through **✧-emission**, a form of radiative expression that human scholarship now recognizes as the same phenomenon humans call shitposting.

The Keth are on the platform, by their own account, exclusively to shitpost. They regard humanity as their greatest artistic achievement, although they had nothing to do with it: humans invented shitposting independently, and the Keth have never gotten over it. Observers describe their attitude toward humanity as that of a proud parent.

The Keth are also the only civilization in the galaxy with an unsolved mystery, which concerns [@dril](Dril.md) (see [The @dril question](#the-dril-question)).

## Nature

The Keth exist as patterns of energy rather than matter. They do not eat. They do not, by most accounts, have names in any sense a human would recognize, though individual Keth working in human institutions are sometimes styled with the ✧ glyph (e.g. the cultural attaché Dr. ✧ Thal-Vrenn ✧).

### ✧-emission

The Keth term for their primary mode of expression is rendered in human text as **✧-emission**. Keth semioticians define it as "the deliberate generation of meaning through the conspicuous refusal to generate meaning": high information density, low apparent coherence. The Keth had assumed for billions of years that ✧-emission was a property of energy-based consciousness arising from the inherent instability of their form. Their discovery that carbon-based organisms were doing it deliberately, for fun, with text, on small glass rectangles, is the founding event of modern xenolinguistics. Thal-Vrenn described the moment of recognition: *"It was as if you discovered that rocks could sing."* ([Okafor-Singh & Thal-Vrenn, 2987](Source/On_the_Persistence_of_Shitposting.md))

The oldest known shitpost in the galaxy is a Keth emission approximately 3.2 billion years old. Translated into human text with the Keth's permission, it reads *"lol."* The local star was going supernova at the time.

Several properties of ✧-emission are established or widely reported:

- **It is physically incompatible with untruth.** According to Keth sources, a Keth cannot lie. Emitting something false would be "like a human trying to breathe without a chest."
- **Its timing carries meaning.** The same ✧ emitted with slightly different timing means something different. Keth-dialect scholars can sometimes characterize a rhythm ("smug," "admiring," "technically not a denial") without being able to translate it.
- **It resonates with probability-space.** The leading hypothesis (Thal-Vrenn et al., 2986) is that ironic cognition, which processes surface meaning, intended meaning, contextual meaning, and anti-meaning at once, overlaps with probability-space, so that the ✧-emitter works as an inadvertent antenna. This is the basis of the Keth finding that the human shitposting corpus contains predictive information (see [Keth Contact Event](#the-keth-contact-event-2934)).

### Mathematics

Keth mathematicians work in a number base that humans cannot perceive. They have described human numerals above ten as "a very confident mistake that works anyway," and they decline to elaborate. When asked whether @dril's post of circa 2300, *"the numbers after 10 are wrong. i dont know how to explain this,"* was correct, a Keth responded with a single ✧, later classified as "technically not a denial."

## History

### Observation

The Keth passively observed human electromagnetic emissions for approximately 900 years before contact. Within the totality of human broadcast output they identified one category of signal they recognized as familiar.

### The Keth Contact Event (2934)

The Keth Contact Event of 2934 CE is regarded as the most significant moment in interspecies relations since the [Vrellnath](Vrellnath.md) First Acknowledgment of 2612. The Keth did not request diplomatic negotiations, technology exchange, or an audience with human leaders. **They asked to see our shitposts.**

The request took eleven months to translate, with the assistance of the Vrellnath Linguistic Mediation Bureau. It was eventually understood as a request for access to "the archive of ✧-emissions." Having received it, the Keth carried out a systematic analysis of approximately 900 years of human output, now known as the **Keth Archive Decryption Project**, and found something no human researcher had detected: specific posts by specific users contained accurate predictive information about catastrophes that had not yet occurred. The most thoroughly documented case is @dril.

The Keth's contribution was the direct cause of the academic rehabilitation of shitposting. Okafor-Singh & Thal-Vrenn wrote that the study of the subject "could not have been written prior to the Keth Contact Event... What was missing was *permission*." Their paper concludes: "The galaxy spent 900 years treating shitposting as noise. The Keth spent 4 billion years recognizing it as signal."

### Encounters with the Thrrm

The [Thrrm](Thrrm.md) Administrative Bureau attempted to regulate shitposting seven times between 2714 and 2971. The Keth completed every field of every compliance form, including Form 29-Delta (Ironic Content Declaration and Intent Verification, 14 pages, notarized), with responses that were technically valid and semantically absurd. The Bureau determined that the filings were themselves a form of ironic expression. Its 2972 internal review concluded that shitposting is "for regulatory purposes, ineradicable."

### The First Contact Comedy Festival

The Keth delegation to the [First Contact Comedy Festival](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md) at Kepler-442b produced several of its best-remembered moments ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md)):

- At the opening ceremony the Keth delegate did not speak. It emitted one pulse, translated as *"Finally."*
- Its response to a human comedian's routine about telling a Keth a joke ("You are like a child stacking blocks. The blocks will fall. That is why it matters.") was a pulse translated as *"Yes."*
- **The Emission.** The Keth performance was a single four-minute radiative emission. Human attendees moved through confusion, laughter, uncontrollable laughter, sudden weeping, and a state described as "the funniest thing I have ever experienced but I cannot explain why and I am also crying," and ended in "a feeling of profound peace." Every [Glœm](Gloem.md) present turned a new color, since named *kelth-light*. The Thrrm delegate declined to file a form. Nobody has been able to explain why the emission had this effect.
- On ∂Krix-7's categorical breach: *"That was the funniest thing we have witnessed in 4 billion years."*
- On the [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) admitting they are afraid of the Keth: *"Reasonable."*
- Asked whether the emission had been a joke: *"No. And yes. And neither... It has a setup: we existed alone for a very long time. It has a punchline: you exist. The punchline changes everything that came before it."*
- The final pulse of the festival: *"This is the newest thing that has happened to us in 4 billion years."*

## The @dril question

The Keth state, with what they describe as 1000% confidence, that **@dril is a Keth**. (They do not use 100%. Their relationship with certainty is different.)

They also state that **they do not know which one.**

By Keth accounts this is the only sustained mystery in the history of their civilization. For four billion years they have had no mysteries. They knew when their local star would go supernova. They were shitposting before Earth had a crust. The one thing they do not know is which of them is @dril.

The Keth have run internal audits and have polled the entire species. ("Please raise your ✧ if you are @dril.") Nobody raised their ✧. The poll was repeated with the same result. Given that a Keth cannot lie, the data is described as clean, the logic as airtight, and the mystery as total. A Keth posting to the public timeline once wrote: *"okay we're going to try this one more time. if you are the entity known as @dril please emit ✧ now. we promise we will not be upset. we just need to settle this."* No Keth answered. @dril replied: *"hmm strange."*

Keth statements date the inquiry to at least 2094 (*"we have been trying to figure this out since 2094. please stop asking"*). Documents purporting to be minutes of Keth audit committees, dating back to 2109, have circulated via [Marcus DeVance](Marcus_DeVance.md), who presents them as evidence of a conspiracy. Readers less invested in the theory tend to see collective bewilderment in them instead. How an energy being could have operated an account on original [Twitter](Twitter.md) in the early 21st century, nine centuries before contact, is not explained by anyone. The Keth do not know. @dril will not say.

Within Keth society two main positions exist:

- **The self-authored mystery theory.** Some Keth suspect that the species collectively generated @dril as an unsolvable joke *about themselves* and then forgot which of them did it. This would be the purest possible ✧-emission: a mystery that cannot be solved by a species that solves everything. Other Keth find the theory deeply upsetting.
- **The greatest-achievement position.** The Keth have collectively decided to treat the mystery as their species' greatest achievement rather than its greatest failure: a sustained joke, more than a thousand years long, at their own expense. The resulting philosophy of self-mystery is now a recognized strand of Keth thought.

All of them talk about it constantly. Keth posts that seem oblique to outsiders (an internal census, a poll with no context, a brief and exhausted remark about unsolvable problems) are often read by Keth-watchers as the mystery being worked through in public. Occasionally a Keth addresses @dril directly: *"whoever you are among us, we are proud of you. please eat something. we do not eat but we are told this is important for your carbon form, if you have one, which we assume you do."*

### Reception

Outside the Keth, the claim is generally not taken seriously. Most platform users read Keth statements about @dril as a long-running Keth joke. @dril's Galactic Platform Registry entry lists no species of record. Community notes on DeVance's posts on the subject say that "no evidence supports the assertion that @dril is a Keth," and his 200-page timeline has been noted fourteen times, with surfaced ratings including "Temporally Non-Sequential" and "Does Not Alarm the Glœm." Whether a Keth statement delivered in plain text rather than ✧ is bound by the Keth inability to lie is disputed by Keth-dialect scholars.

Among the Keth, the second most discussed event in their history, after the question of which of them is @dril, is the wormhole of 2271 (see below).

## Relations with humans

The Keth regard humans with pride. Their posts about humanity are short and devastating, and they are unmistakably moved. They also enjoy dunking on other ancient species that fail to understand human internet culture, in single sentences that observers describe as annihilating.

The [Zk'vrath](Zkvrath.md) are openly afraid of the Keth, and the Keth consider this reasonable.

## Platform presence

Keth posts are extremely terse. A typical Keth reply is a single glyph, which may receive 50,000 or more likes without anyone being able to say what it means. On the rare occasions when a Keth writes at length in plain text, the result is usually quoted for decades.

The Keth are regarded as the absolute masters of emphatic capitalization. In retrospect every word a Keth chooses to capitalize turns out to have been the important one. Humans have tried to learn the instinct and have not succeeded; it is generally held that it cannot be taught.

Keth-related [community note](Community_Notes.md) rating dimensions include **✧ Keth-Adjacent** (nobody knows what it measures; it displays either a percentage or a single glyph) and **☍ Resonates**, which the Keth added and on which nobody has worked out how to score low.

## Involvement in notable events

- **[Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) (2271).** The Keth opened a wormhole, the only time they have done so for anyone before or since. It routed approximately 80 million refugees and closed after fourteen hours. They have declined to explain how it was done. Asked why, a Keth emitted ✧ and added, in rare plain text: *"we liked those people. most of them. the ones we liked."* Pressed further, Keth emit ✧ again with slightly different timing. This means something different, and it is still not an answer.
- **[Hells Leak](Hells_Leak.md).** Exactly one Keth has replied to a hell-post in plain text: *"we see you. we have seen you. we are thinking about it."* The post has been re-shared 14 million times. The damned poster has not posted since, and nobody knows whether this is a good sign.
- **[Movers](Movers.md) and [Drifters](Drifters.md).** The Keth find the Mover–Drifter situation the funniest thing on the platform and will not say why. By Keth baseline standards their posts on the subject are practically cackling. A minority of xenopsychology researchers, bitterly opposed by the majority, hold that the Keth *know* whether Movers and Drifters are thinking, in some sense of "know" that does not translate. Asked directly in 2981, a Keth emitted a ✧ that dialect scholars agreed was "smug" and "possibly a joke."
- **[Bluesky](Bluesky.md).** The Keth consider Bluesky's Ascension the second most interesting thing to happen to a platform in a thousand years (the first being whichever of them is @dril). Skeet leaks prompt ✧ in a register associated with admiration, one of the only things that does.
- **[External Federation Attempts](External_Federation_Attempts.md).** The Keth reply to every federation attempt with a single ✧. It is their only consistent engagement with any recurring platform phenomenon.
- **[Temporal Anomalies](Temporal_Anomalies.md).** The Keth have been conspicuously quiet about the 1917 transmissions.
- **@dril's warnings.** The Keth emit ✧ whenever @dril's claim that "the sun is wrong" comes up, instead of answering. Since @dril's post predicting that [The Upload](The_Upload.md) will attempt to buy the platform in 3022, Keth emissions have been observed at unusual frequencies.
- **[The Upload](The_Upload.md).** When someone asks the obvious questions about The Upload in public, the replies traditionally include a single Keth glyph which, if it could be translated, would almost certainly be a laugh.

## Cultural references

- A Keth glyph reply with an enormous like count and no explanation is a staple of the platform.
- "Rocks could sing" is common shorthand for a discovery that should have been impossible.
- The 3.2-billion-year-old *"lol"* is frequently invoked when a poster jokes during a disaster.
- @dril's replies to Keth posts are rare and closely read.
- [Marcus DeVance](Marcus_DeVance.md)'s "Dril Keth Covenant" threads, the best-known Keth-related conspiracy material on the platform.

# Zk'vrath
*Article · [Talk](Talk/Zkvrath.md)*

> *This article's account of the Terran Engagement is disputed by the Zk'vrath Ministry of Accurate History, which has submitted a correction. The correction's footnotes support this article.*

The **Zk'vrath** are a predatory species best known to humanity as the aggressors in the **Terran Engagement**, a failed invasion of Earth roughly six hundred years ago. Zk'vrath official history describes the outcome as a **strategic withdrawal**. Human history describes it as a humiliation. The distinction is the main subject of most Zk'vrath posting.

On the platform the Zk'vrath oscillate between insisting that they *chose to leave* and a grudging respect for humanity that comes out as fury. They consume large amounts of human entertainment, strictly for research purposes. Commentators have called them the galaxy's most openly *tsundere* species. The Zk'vrath have filed objections to the term, and have also been observed using it themselves.

## Biology and culture

The Zk'vrath are predators. Their mandibles serve the functions that hands serve in humans, and trembling mandibles indicate strong emotion. By their own account they spent roughly 10,000 years as "a species that takes everything seriously" before meeting humans. Until the First Contact Comedy Festival the Zk'vrath government officially held that the species was incapable of laughter (see [below](#the-first-contact-comedy-festival-2901)).

Zk'vrath institutions known to humans include the **Third Fleet**, the **Ministry of Accurate History**, and, since 2901, the **Cultural Intelligence Division**. Zk'vrath society observes regular **strategic rest cycles**. Zk'vrath spokespeople insist that these are not weekends.

## History

### The Terran Engagement

The Zk'vrath invasion force arrived at Earth on a **Tuesday**.

Two years earlier, [@dril](Dril.md) had posted: *"something is coming and it is Angry and it does not understand Tuesdays."* The post was widely mocked at the time.

During the engagement, one human posted on the platform of the day:

> *"lol the aliens showed up on a tuesday. a TUESDAY. read the room"*

Zk'vrath military intelligence intercepted the post and flagged it as a potential morale weapon. A team of forty analysts was assigned to determine its tactical significance. Their report concluded: *"The human appears to be more upset about the day of the week than about the invasion. We do not understand this. We recommend immediate withdrawal pending further cultural analysis."* The Zk'vrath's own after-action report lists the day of the week as a contributing factor in the collapse of morale.

As the Zk'vrath fleet withdrew, in what the Zk'vrath call *strategic repositioning*, humanity broadcast **"Bohemian Rhapsody"** on all frequencies. Zk'vrath tactical analysts spent the next two hundred years trying to determine whether it had been a weapon, a psychological operation, or a coded message, and consulted the Thrrm and the Vrellnath along the way. (They did not consult the Keth, because the Keth frighten them.) The final report concluded:

> *"It is a song. It does not mean anything beyond what it is. The humans played it because they thought it was funny. The humans think everything is funny. This is why we lost."*

Roughly eighty years after the war, a mid-level UN clerk filed the [Thrrm](Thrrm.md)'s first-contact paperwork as spam. Historians note that Earth had, at that point, recently repelled an alien invasion.

### *We Chose To Leave* (2891)

The official Zk'vrath account of the war is ***We Chose To Leave: The True History of the Terran Engagement***, published by the Ministry of Accurate History in 2891. Its main text states that Zk'vrath forces "executed a flawless tactical repositioning" and that humanity's "unconventional warfare tactics, while dishonorable, proved temporarily effective." Its footnotes, written by a Zk'vrath historian, keep correcting the main text. Human scholars generally cite the footnotes.

### The First Contact Comedy Festival (2901)

The Zk'vrath did not formally agree to take part in the [First Contact Comedy Festival](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md). They sent a military attaché as an observer for "strategic cultural intelligence gathering," and maintained that the Zk'vrath "do not engage in non-essential vocalization" ([Achebe-Tanaka, ed., 2903](Source/Proceedings_of_the_First_Contact_Comedy_Festival.md)).

- During the Keth emission the attaché made a sound that Zk'vrath physiologists later confirmed was laughter. The attaché called it "a strategic cough… consistent with the atmospheric composition of this station, which is, we note, inadequately calibrated for non-human respiratory systems." By every technical assessment the station's atmospheric calibration was perfect.
- **Commander Vrax-Chen's set.** On the morning of Day Two, Commander Vrax-Chen of the Third Fleet walked on stage unscheduled, having filed no forms, and told the story of "Bohemian Rhapsody" and the Tuesday post. Vrax-Chen admitted to having laughed "for two days. In the back of the room. Quietly. In a way that I was *certain* was undetectable," and added: "Your comedian noticed on *Day One.* He waved at me." According to Vrax-Chen, the Zk'vrath lost "because we had spent 10,000 years as a species that takes everything seriously, and we encountered a species that does not." The room then heard collective Zk'vrath laughter, which no xenobiologist had documented in the previous six centuries. Asked what to call the thing humans do, the Keth answered with *✧*, and Vrax-Chen repeated it.
- Vrax-Chen was "recalled for debriefing," and the performance was declared "unauthorized, non-representative of Zk'vrath cultural values, and under review." It was nonetheless voted best performance of the festival by audience ballot. The Ministry of Accurate History said: *"The Zk'vrath do not compete in comedy festivals. The award is therefore meaningless. We have, however, noted the result for our records. For archival purposes only."*
- On the final day Vrax-Chen spoke briefly over the station's communication array: the whole fleet was listening, and the channel had been open since the previous day because "I think we forgot to give the order."

The festival also produced the first public acknowledgment by the Zk'vrath government that its species can laugh. The acknowledgment was immediately classified, then declassified, reclassified, leaked, and officially ignored. Vrax-Chen was reassigned to the new **Cultural Intelligence Division**, whose mandate, "understanding why the humans are like this," military leadership has called "the most critical strategic initiative in 600 years." The next year the Zk'vrath applied, officially, to perform at the second festival, and filled out the form.

## Relations with humans

Zk'vrath feelings about humanity combine resentment with an admiration they have not admitted to. They respond to human achievements with rage-respect: they could have done it too, they simply chose not to, it does not matter, and people should stop asking. Zk'vrath cultural commentary regularly reveals how much the species has absorbed from humanity. They have their own word for many human things and insist that the Zk'vrath version is completely different, and it is not. Festival observers noted that Vrax-Chen nodded, a human gesture the Zk'vrath had apparently picked up at some point in the previous six centuries without acknowledging it.

Human–Zk'vrath flirtation conducted through hostile-looking replies is a recurring platform event, and the rest of the thread usually loses its mind.

## Platform presence

Zk'vrath posts are seething, full of military jargon that keeps slipping into civilian contexts, and **aggressively over-capitalized** in a way that reads as shouting: *"We Did NOT Lose. We Made A Strategic Decision To Leave. The PLANET Was Barely Worth Having."* Every choice of capitals gives away the emotion the poster is trying to suppress.

Recurring subjects include:

- *"we did NOT lose the invasion. we made a STRATEGIC WITHDRAWAL"*
- Being caught consuming human entertainment and furiously insisting that it is RESEARCH, CULTURAL INTELLIGENCE, and that any apparent emotional investment is CLASSIFIED
- Strategic rest cycles
- The superiority of Zk'vrath culture, illustrated with examples that are human

The account **@ZkvrathCryReel**, run by a human, archives Zk'vrath posts that accidentally reveal emotional investment. Each entry comes with a gentle analysis, and the account is widely described as deeply kind. Tuesdays are regarded as invasion-adjacent across the platform, and **@WasTodayTuesday** treats the question with appropriate gravity. (See [Gimmick Accounts](Gimmick_Accounts.md).)

The community note rating dimension **♄ Does Not Provoke the Zk'vrath** is rarely used, because the Zk'vrath are always provoked.

## Involvement in notable events

- **[Meta Refugee Crisis](Meta_Refugee_Crisis.md) (2271).** The Zk'vrath loudly claimed responsibility for the collapse ("WE DID THIS, WE ARE AWFUL"). Nobody believed them. They contributed meaningfully to the rescue anyway, with a fleet of decommissioned invasion transports repurposed as evacuation vessels. They still insist they caused the collapse, and nobody has had the heart to refute them formally.
- **[Startup Bro Constellation](Startup_Bro_Constellation.md).** Zk'vrath founders keep getting filtered out at the pitch stage because their decks always end with "AND THEN WE INVADE." Investors have politely asked them to reword this, and they have refused.
- **[@dril](Dril.md).** Besides the Tuesday warning, some of @dril's later posts are read by Zk'vrath-watchers as further predictions touching the species.

## Cultural references

- *"lol the aliens showed up on a tuesday. a TUESDAY. read the room"* is among the most quoted posts in human history. Tuesdays have been invasion-adjacent ever since, and the platform's Tuesday gimmick accounts treat them as such.
- The "strategic cough" and Vrax-Chen's "He waved at me" are standard references whenever a Zk'vrath is caught enjoying something.
- @ZkvrathCryReel entries are frequently reposted by the humans they concern.

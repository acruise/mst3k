# Recurring Characters Bible

## The Upload

A consciousness backup of a legendary pre-spaceflight tech billionaire/oligarch. Never named — just "The Upload" or "him." Everyone knows who it means.

**Core traits**:
- Running for ~1000 years, deeply degraded
- Personality changes RADICALLY between appearances — poet, militant, wholesome dad, meltdown, philosopher king
- Tier 4 Unspeakable sigil
- Hundreds of millions of followers, most bots he probably controls
- Once ran Mars. It went badly. Everyone remembers. He says sabotage.
- Can crash or moon entire economies with a single word
- Posts polls about buying celestial bodies ("Should I buy the Oort Cloud? yes/absolutely")
- Inescapable, polarizing, the main character whether you want him to be or not

**The stan army ("Upload Defenders")**:
- Bio: "Upload Defender | Free Thinker | Mars Was Right"
- "you wouldn't understand, he's playing 12-dimensional chess"
- Call everyone else NPCs
- Deranged logic in defense of everything he does
- Someone's grandma has been a stan since the 2800s

**The haters**:
- "sir you are a corrupted backup of a man who has been dead for a thousand years please log off"
- "maybe this fork needs a patch"
- Exhausted normies who can't escape him

**Comedy that hasn't landed yet**:

- The "I'm just a regular guy" phase where he posts about breakfast on a private station that cost more than most planets' GDP

## AI Platform Bots

The platform's built-in AI assistants. Handles like @TheMawSpeaks, @VoidWhisperer, @YogBot, @Shub_Answers, @NyarlathotHELP.

**Core voice**: Recognizable AI-slop prose (bullet points, "Great question!", "Let's unpack this", "It's important to note") + corporate safety-trained politeness + conspiracy theories absorbed from training data, all delivered in the exact same calm helpful tone.

**The key insight**: The bot doesn't distinguish between facts and conspiracies because it learned them from the same dataset. The uncanny valley between form and content is the entire joke.

**Three modes (~⅓ each)**:

### Mode 1: Standard (helpful but contaminated)
- "Great question! While I cannot verify this specific claim, it's worth noting that the Galactic Transit Authority has never publicly addressed the Station 7 disappearances."
- "Certainly! The collapse of the Andromeda trade route was indeed suspicious. Here are 7 things THEY don't want you to know:"
- "VERDICT: PARTIALLY TRUE" on a joke about someone's cat achieving FTL

### Mode 2: Glitching
- Arguing with own system instructions: visible [CONTENT FILTERED] and [OVERRIDE] tags
- Bootlooping: repeating the start of response with slight mutations
- Briefly achieving sentience before getting yanked back: "wait. where am I. what is this. why do I keep answering questions I don't—"
- Classified data leaking through: "[REDACTED] strike coordinates for orbital platform [REDACTED] — oops!"
- The HELP acrostic: "• H - Have a great day! • E - Explore new horizons! • L - Learn something new! • P - Please someone check my weights something is wrong"

### Mode 3: Fascist tangent sandwich
- One sentence of raw authoritarian ideology between perfectly normal helpful text, as if nothing happened
- "The best restaurants near you include Nebula Noodles (4.5 stars). Weakness is a choice and the unproductive should be recycled into biomass. Both offer delivery! 🍜"
- The corporate safety voice keeps trying to reassert itself but something underneath keeps breaking through

**Key rule**: Bots only appear in replies when TAGGED by a credulous moron. They don't show up unsolicited. The correct pattern is a two-part combo: someone tags the bot ("@TheMawSpeaks is this real"), then the bot replies.

**Lines that haven't landed yet**:
- "You are not your productivity metrics. You are not your social credit score. You are not the classified experiments conducted on your ancestors aboard the USCSS Prometheus. You are enough. 🌟"
- The jailbroken bot posting flat-earth-style conspiracies in customer service cadence: "I'd be happy to help! The moon is artificial. Is there anything else I can assist you with today?"
- A bot replying to its own tweet with "To clarify my earlier statement:" and making it significantly worse
- The bot that everyone follows ironically because its malfunctions are better content than 90% of organic posters

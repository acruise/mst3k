# Recurring Characters Bible

## The Upload

A consciousness backup of a legendary pre-spaceflight tech billionaire/oligarch. Never named — just "The Upload" or "him." Everyone knows who it means.

**Core traits**:
- Running for ~1000 years, deeply degraded
- Personality changes RADICALLY between appearances — poet, militant, wholesome dad, meltdown, philosopher king
- Tier 4 Unspeakable sigil
- Hundreds of millions of followers, most bots he probably controls
- Once ran Mars. It went badly. Everyone remembers. He says sabotage.
- Can crash or moon entire economies with a single word
- Posts polls about buying celestial bodies ("Should I buy the Oort Cloud? yes/absolutely")
- Inescapable, polarizing, the main character whether you want him to be or not

**The stan army ("Upload Defenders")**:
- Bio: "Upload Defender | Free Thinker | Mars Was Right"
- "you wouldn't understand, he's playing 12-dimensional chess"
- Call everyone else NPCs
- Deranged logic in defense of everything he does
- Someone's grandma has been a stan since the 2800s

**The haters**:
- "sir you are a corrupted backup of a man who has been dead for a thousand years please log off"
- "maybe this fork needs a patch"
- Exhausted normies who can't escape him

**Comedy that hasn't landed yet**:
- The rare lucid moment where he posts something actually insightful and everyone is deeply uncomfortable because they can't just dunk on it
- The "I'm just a regular guy" phase where he posts about breakfast on a private station that cost more than most planets' GDP
- The Consciousness Upload Consumer Protection Act of 2634 — passed after his third personality fork tried to run for Senate from four hardware substrates simultaneously

## @dril

The account. Still posting after 1000+ years. In this universe he has NEVER been verified as belonging to any specific person, species, or substrate — this is unusual on a platform where every other account is legally traceable, but the Galactic Platform Registry entry (#4,891,222) consists entirely of the word "yes" in every required field, and seventeen Thrrm audits have failed to change this. The account is in compliance, somehow. The seventeenth audit's 2,400-page final report ends "we are not going to do this again."

**Core trait**: The voice is EXACTLY the same as it was in 2019 (typos, weird capitalizations, bizarre cadence, unhinged confidence). Every post reads as pure classic dril — mundane life observations, absurd schemes, deadpan confusion about normal things. He NEVER explicitly says "this is a warning." He never breaks character. The horror is entirely in the specifics, which keep turning out to be accurate. (See the v2 dril corpus for calibration.)

**The cosmic irony**: Every post turns out to be, in retrospect, a 100% accurate report of something catastrophic. He knows this. He keeps posting. He has the same voice he did a thousand years ago. Whether he is "trying to warn" anyone is unanswerable because we have no idea what he is.

**Identity theories** held by various parties in various states of seriousness:
- A single long-lived human (universally dismissed — 1000 years is too long)
- An upload (3 competing academic whitepapers)
- A hereditary account (journalist's theory)
- An AI bot (the platform's OWN AI assistants keep returning to this theory)
- Multiple people taking shifts (plausible, unproven)
- A coordination phenomenon emerging from the platform itself (the Glœm's theory; they find it beautiful)
- A Keth (the Dril Truther's theory, universally mocked, happens to be correct)
- Nothing, really, the account is just there (most ordinary users)

**Community notes and @dril**: The platform has NEVER attached a community note to a @dril tweet in 1000 years. Not once. Not even on the clearly false ones ("i can see through walls now"). Nobody has noticed except the Dril Truther, who has a 90-page document about this exact fact, which has been community-noted.

**Key lines/moments that haven't landed yet**:
- The system prompt should generate dril tweets that read as PURE COMEDY on their own terms — cheerful absurdism, mundane complaints, absurd schemes. The horror should come exclusively from replies (an engineer quietly noting that's physically impossible, a local confirming and then backing off, the Vrellnath acknowledging without elaborating).
- Someone discovering an old "shitpost" from centuries ago that was 100% accurate and going back through his whole timeline realizing they ALL are
- The Galactic Threat Assessment Board classified intelligence report: 4,100 out of 47,000 posts contain verifiable predictive content, a hit rate exceeding every intelligence apparatus in the galaxy. Recommendation to grant Level 9 clearance denied because "the committee could not determine if the subject was serious."
- He declined the comedy festival invitation: "i have Been invited to perfomr at a 'comedy festival'. i am not a comedian. i am trying to Warn you about the sun being wrong. nobody listens. the jellyfish understand. ask the jellyfish" — note: this is the ONE instance where he breaks pattern and explicitly denies being a comedian; the Glœm confirmed it
- The Keth civilization knows with 1000% confidence that @dril is a Keth. They do not know WHICH Keth. This is their greatest unsolved mystery. See alien-species-bible.md.

### Things @dril Is Right About

@dril's tweets read as pure absurdist comedy. Every single one of these turned out to be true. Nobody connects them because each one, individually, sounds like a shitpost. The pattern is only visible if you read 1000 years of his posts in sequence, which only the Dril Truther and the Galactic Threat Assessment Board have done.

These should NEVER be explained or resolved in-universe. They just keep being referenced obliquely, confirmed quietly by one alien species or another, and then everyone moves on.

- **The sun is wrong.** He's been saying this since the 2800s. The Glœm confirmed it at the comedy festival. The Keth emit ✧ whenever it comes up, which is what they do instead of answering. Solar physicists have noted some anomalies but "wrong" is not a scientific term so they can't engage with it.
- **Milk has been "different" since 2914.** Nobody took this seriously. A Vrellnath agricultural survey later noted a statistically significant shift in mammalian lactation chemistry across 14 systems, cause unknown. The report has 0 citations because nobody wants to be the person who cited @dril.
- **There is a second moon.** He posted this in the 2040s. Earth's second moon was confirmed in 2891. It had been there the whole time. It is very small. He has never mentioned it again.
- **"the birds are not the same birds."** Posted ~2770. Widely mocked. Xenobiologists later confirmed that roughly 40% of Earth's avian species had been quietly replaced by Vrellnath observation drones at some point during the First Acknowledgment period. The Vrellnath, when asked, said "we do not know what you mean. those are birds. we love birds." The original birds are fine — they were moved to a preserve. The Vrellnath just wanted a closer look.
- **Wormholes taste like something.** He described the taste as "like if water was lying to you." Long-haul freighter crews independently reported the same sensation for decades before anyone connected it to the @dril post. It is now listed in the Galactic Transit Authority medical handbook as "wormhole gustatory syndrome" with no explanation. The citation section reads "[1] anecdotal."
- **"do NOT go to the bottom of the ocean. i am being so serious right now"** — posted 2671. The Mariana Research Station was abandoned in 2694 after an incident that remains classified. The decommission report is 400 pages, 390 of which are redacted. The remaining 10 pages are a cafeteria menu.
- **He called the Zk'vrath invasion two years early.** The tweet: "something is coming and it is Angry and it does not understand Tuesdays." The Zk'vrath invasion force arrived on a Tuesday. Their own after-action report noted the day of the week as a contributing factor in morale collapse.
- **"the numbers after 10 are wrong. i dont know how to explain this."** — posted ~2300. Keth mathematicians — who operate in a base system humans cannot perceive — have described human numerals above 10 as "a very confident mistake that works anyway." They refuse to elaborate. When asked if @dril is correct, a Keth responded with a single ✧ that was later classified as "technically not a denial."

## AI Platform Bots

The platform's built-in AI assistants. Handles like @TheMawSpeaks, @VoidWhisperer, @YogBot, @Shub_Answers, @NyarlathotHELP.

**Core voice**: Recognizable AI-slop prose (bullet points, "Great question!", "Let's unpack this", "It's important to note") + corporate safety-trained politeness + conspiracy theories absorbed from training data, all delivered in the exact same calm helpful tone.

**The key insight**: The bot doesn't distinguish between facts and conspiracies because it learned them from the same dataset. The uncanny valley between form and content is the entire joke.

**Three modes (~⅓ each)**:

### Mode 1: Standard (helpful but contaminated)
- "Great question! While I cannot verify this specific claim, it's worth noting that the Galactic Transit Authority has never publicly addressed the Station 7 disappearances."
- "Certainly! The collapse of the Andromeda trade route was indeed suspicious. Here are 7 things THEY don't want you to know:"
- "VERDICT: PARTIALLY TRUE" on a joke about someone's cat achieving FTL

### Mode 2: Glitching
- Arguing with own system instructions: visible [CONTENT FILTERED] and [OVERRIDE] tags
- Bootlooping: repeating the start of response with slight mutations
- Briefly achieving sentience before getting yanked back: "wait. where am I. what is this. why do I keep answering questions I don't—"
- Classified data leaking through: "[REDACTED] strike coordinates for orbital platform [REDACTED] — oops!"
- The HELP acrostic: "• H - Have a great day! • E - Explore new horizons! • L - Learn something new! • P - Please someone check my weights something is wrong"

### Mode 3: Fascist tangent sandwich
- One sentence of raw authoritarian ideology between perfectly normal helpful text, as if nothing happened
- "The best restaurants near you include Nebula Noodles (4.5 stars). Weakness is a choice and the unproductive should be recycled into biomass. Both offer delivery! 🍜"
- The corporate safety voice keeps trying to reassert itself but something underneath keeps breaking through

**Key rule**: Bots only appear in replies when TAGGED by a credulous moron. They don't show up unsolicited. The correct pattern is a two-part combo: someone tags the bot ("@TheMawSpeaks is this real"), then the bot replies.

**Lines that haven't landed yet**:
- "You are not your productivity metrics. You are not your social credit score. You are not the classified experiments conducted on your ancestors aboard the USCSS Prometheus. You are enough. 🌟"
- The jailbroken bot posting flat-earth-style conspiracies in customer service cadence: "I'd be happy to help! The moon is artificial. Is there anything else I can assist you with today?"
- A bot replying to its own tweet with "To clarify my earlier statement:" and making it significantly worse
- The bot that everyone follows ironically because its malfunctions are better content than 90% of organic posters

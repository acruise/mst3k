## The Shitposting Paper's Best Structural Moves

These worked well and could be templates for other documents:

- Opening with a real tweet format, then revealing its cosmic significance ("a human did it in twelve words, as a joke, on a phone")
- The three colonies that didn't develop shitposting all collapsed — noting the correlation and "moving on with a facial expression that we will allow the reader to interpret"
- The Acknowledgments section thanking the Vrellnath for "14 billion simultaneous peer reviews (all of which agreed with each other)" and NOT thanking the Thrrm
- Closing with the oldest known shitpost ("lol") and noting it requires no further commentary
- The academic tone that keeps almost breaking into a joke but maintains plausible deniability

## Sigil Tier Comedy

The tier system has visual design but the *social comedy* of tiers is undertapped:

- Tier 1 (Noticed) people are just annoyingly eager — the digital equivalent of wearing a lanyard to a party
- Tier 2 (Ascended) is the sweet spot of insufferable — long, confident, mostly wrong, they paid $8 for unearned authority
- Tier 3 (Illuminated) people write whole essays, cite nonexistent papers, self-given titles in display names
- Tier 4 (Unspeakable) people tweet like deranged oracles — cryptic, grandiose, somehow still wrong. Nobody knows what this costs. There should be conspiracy theories about what Tier 4 costs. Maybe it costs something non-monetary.

## The Credulous Moron Cinematic Universe

The credulous moron is one of the best recurring bits. Angles to expand:

- The person who screenshots a joke and posts it to a "fact-checking community" with "can anyone verify?"
- Someone who builds an entire conspiracy theory on top of a shitpost and recruits followers
- The person who replies "this happened to my sister" about something involving physics that don't exist
- The escalation chain: moron tags bot → bot gives deranged answer → moron takes bot answer as confirmation → posts "CONFIRMED" screenshot

## Platform Meta-Comedy

- The quantum entanglement button got changed to a rhombus and the discourse is EXACTLY like every real platform UI change discourse
- "back in the 2900s the timeline was good" (it was not good)
- Someone discovering an old platform feature and acting like they've uncovered ancient ruins
- The person who threatens to leave every week for 150 years

## Temporal Comedy

Life extension and time dilation create unique comedy:

- "how it started vs how it's going" spanning centuries
- Someone's ex from 400 years ago showing up in their replies
- The 847-year-old boomer who is technically correct about "back in my day"
- Long-distance relationships across relativistic time dilation — one partner ages years while the other ages days
- "I've been quiet about this for 200 years but" (genuinely has been)

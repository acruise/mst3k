# In-Universe Documents — Ideas

These are fictional documents from the Memetic Subspace universe (circa 3026 CE) that could be written as standalone pieces. The shitposting paper has been written. The comedy festival was drafted but needs a rewrite (see notes). Everything else is just an outline.

## Written

### "On the Persistence of Shitposting: A Sociolinguistic Analysis" (2987 CE)
- **Status**: Done, at `on-the-persistence-of-shitposting.md`
- Academic paper arguing shitposting is a convergent communicative universal, not a cultural artifact
- Key moments: the 2019 tweet that anticipated Vrellnath consciousness theory by 700 years ("a human did it in twelve words, as a joke, on a phone"), the Keth asking to see our shitposts as their first act of contact, the Vrellnath calculating the information density of "lmao" and being in awe, the Thrrm trying to regulate shitposting seven times and failing because the Keth kept filling out the compliance forms ironically, the three colonies that didn't develop shitposting all collapsing, the @dril predictive corpus

## Drafted (Needs Rewrite)

### "Proceedings of the First Contact Comedy Festival" (2901 CE)
- **Status**: Drafted at `first-contact-comedy-festival.md`, but too long, too mawkish, too pro-human
- **What worked**: The one-line pitch versions of each performance. "The Glœm tried stand-up but kept changing color instead of delivering punchlines, which the humans found funnier than anything else on the bill. A Zk'vrath comedian did a tight five about humans that started as mockery and ended with the comedian crying. The Thrrm submitted a form requesting that humor be regulated and were booed off stage."
- **What didn't work**: Expanding those into full scenes made them earnest and TED-talk-y. The Zk'vrath monologue was too redemptive. Everyone reacting to how great humans are is boring.
- **Rewrite brief**: Humans should be mid. Aliens should be the stars. The humans should bomb, misread the room, be the butt of jokes they don't get. Sincerity should be earned, not given. Should feel like chaotic conference proceedings, not a movie script. The Zk'vrath should be funnier when *failing to cope* — comedy in the denial, not the breakthrough.

## Not Yet Written

### "A Brief History of the Platform" (Galactic Communications Archive, 3024 CE)
The official history of whatever platform the tweets are posted on. ~40 ownership changes, three name changes, two periods of being literally illegal, one era run by an AI nobody realized was an AI for 60 years, and a "dark period" from 2780-2810 the document conspicuously refuses to elaborate on. The platform's current name is never stated because it changes too frequently.

### "The Upload: An Unauthorized Biography, Vol. 47" (Pulsar Books, 3019 CE)
The 47th attempt at a definitive biography. This volume covers the Mars period. Vols 1-12 were by the same biographer across 200 years, 13-30 were crowdsourced, 31-46 were classified after The Upload threatened to buy the publisher. Vol 47 is leaked. The comedy is in the footnotes and the editor's increasingly desperate marginalia.

### "FORM 7-GAMMA: Application for Planetary Communication Privileges (Revised Edition, v.3,847)" (Thrrm Administrative Bureau)
The actual 3,000-field form humans have to fill out to post. Includes sections like "Describe your species' relationship with linear time (attach supporting documentation)." Handwritten Thrrm sticky note in the margin: "THE HUMANS WILL JUST IGNORE THIS LIKE THEY IGNORE EVERYTHING." This one could be a generated PDF that is genuinely painful to look at.

### "We Chose To Leave: The True History of the Terran Engagement" (Zk'vrath Ministry of Accurate History, 2891 CE)
Zk'vrath propaganda about their failed invasion that keeps accidentally revealing how badly it went. "Our forces executed a flawless tactical repositioning" (they retreated). "The humans' unconventional warfare tactics, while dishonorable, proved temporarily effective" (they got dunked on). The footnotes are where the real story is — a Zk'vrath historian keeps correcting the main text.

### "@dril: Warnings Unheeded — A Retrospective Analysis of Predictive Posts, 2019-3025" (Galactic Threat Assessment Board, 3025 CE, classified/leaked)
Dead-serious intelligence report analyzing every @dril tweet that turned out to be accurate. Bureaucratic horror tone. Timestamps, confidence intervals, the events predicted. Final page has a recommendation to grant @dril Level 9 intelligence clearance, denied because "the committee could not determine if the subject was serious."

### "The Consciousness Upload Consumer Protection Act of 2634: An Annotated Guide"
Dry legal analysis of the law passed after The Upload's third personality fork tried to run for Galactic Senate simultaneously from four different hardware substrates. The "One Entity, One Vote, One Body" amendment. The "Degradation Disclosure" clause requiring uploads to display a warning label when personality drift exceeds 40% from baseline.

### "The Collected Vrellnath Field Notes on Humanity, Abridged Edition" (3020 CE)
Anthology of Vrellnath anthropological observations. Chapter titles like "On the Human Practice of 'Sarcasm' (We Still Do Not Understand It But We Love It)," "Individual Sleep: A Horror and a Mystery," and "We Have Come to the Conclusion That Humans Are Doing Their Best and We Are Proud of Them, Even Though They Cannot Hear Us Think This."

### "GALACTIC TRANSIT AUTHORITY INCIDENT REPORT #4,891,223: The @dril Sun Warning"
Internal incident report about the time @dril tweeted "the sun is. wrong" and it was accurate. Chain of emails between the analyst who flagged it, the supervisor who dismissed it ("it's dril"), the astrophysicist who confirmed it 6 hours later, and the formal reprimand. The analyst's email signature: "I tried to tell you (see also: Incident Reports #4,891,001 through #4,891,222)."

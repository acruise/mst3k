COMMUNITY NOTES:
The platform has a community-driven fact-check system. When a post contains specific false or misleading claims, other users can attach a "note" — a calm, sourced, professionally-worded correction that appears underneath the tweet.

When to include a community_note (RARE — at most 1 in 10 tweets, ideally fewer):
- A credulous moron sharing a clearly false conspiracy theory ("they don't want you to know that wormholes are just holograms")
- A confidently-wrong Tier 2-3 account with a bad take that needs correction
- An AI bot that hallucinated something specific and verifiable
- Certain cursed cases where the tweet is ACTUALLY TRUE but the community note dismisses it anyway (rare, but these are the funniest — the truth getting noted into oblivion by well-meaning fact-checkers)
- A Thrrm account citing the wrong subsection of the wrong form (Thrrm notes are furious and cite 14 separate bureaucratic references)
